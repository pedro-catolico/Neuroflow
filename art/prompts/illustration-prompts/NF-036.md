## 3. PROMPT ARCHITECTURE COMPONENTS (Prompt Architecture v0.1)

### BLOCK A — Identity Constants
*Referência:* `art/prompts/MASTER_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Direção:* Contemplative Figurative identity. Crisp black line art on pure white paper.

### BLOCK B & C — Subject & Structural Setup
- **Subject Description:** A dignified adult male figure standing quietly beneath the sheltered wooden eaves of a rustic temple portal, peacefully observing rainfall outside.
- **Framing & Camera Perspective:** Frontal framing with active timber borders of the portal framing the standing figure and vertical rain lines beyond.
- **Pose / Attitude:** Still, upright posture of shelter, quiet awareness, and introspective pause.

### BLOCK D — Neurographic Integration
- **Line Origin:** Vertical lines originate from falling raindrops, timber columns, and the hem of his robe.
- **Spatial Connection:** Vertical rain streams fall peacefully, framing the portal opening and echoing the upright posture of the observer in a quiet cadence.

### BLOCK E — Composition & Flow Variables
- **Compositional Type:** Bordas ativas (active wooden border framing with clear central viewing portal).
- **Dominant Flow:** Vertical (downward vertical rainfall cadence).
- **Negative Space Allocation:** Approximately 30-35% pure white negative space in the sheltered interior and open rain channels.

### BLOCK F & G — Density, Complexity & Coloring Experience
- **Complexity Level:** Level 2 (Accessible adult complexity).
- **Line Density:** Baixa-Média (low-medium line density, calm and breathable).
- **Coloring Cells:** Clean, bold outlines defining the wooden beam portal and male silhouette, with spacious vertical rainfall ribbons for relaxing coloring.
- **Line Finish:** Pure 100% black line art, zero gray lines, zero grayscale shading, zero gradients, zero solid black fill.

### BLOCK H — Negative Constraints
*Referência:* `art/prompts/NEGATIVE_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Proibições ativas:* No text, watermarks, signatures. No character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, cute/kawaii aesthetic. No samurai combat, theatrical drama, sad depression tropes. No photorealism, 3D render. No rigid geometric mandalas.

Contemplative Figurative adult coloring book page, Level 2 complexity, crisp black line art on pure white paper. A serene adult male figure standing sheltered beneath the rustic wooden portal of a quiet temple, watching gentle rain fall outside, frontal framing.
