## 3. PROMPT ARCHITECTURE COMPONENTS (Prompt Architecture v0.1)

### BLOCK A — Identity Constants
*Referência:* `art/prompts/MASTER_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Direção:* Contemplative Figurative identity. Crisp black line art on pure white paper.

### BLOCK B & C — Subject & Structural Setup
- **Subject Description:** Two elegant adult swans gliding smoothly across mirror-like still water, their graceful arched necks and curved plumage sending resonant ripples outwards.
- **Framing & Camera Perspective:** Elevated three-quarter perspective looking down upon the gliding swans and their concentric watery ripples.
- **Pose / Attitude:** Serene, effortless gliding posture embodying organic grace, rhythm, and tranquility.

### BLOCK D — Neurographic Integration
- **Line Origin:** Curving lines originate from the arched necks of the swans, wing feathers, and water wakes.
- **Spatial Connection:** The sculptural contours of the swans flow seamlessly into concentric, spiral-like water waves, weaving avian form and liquid surface into continuous pulses.

### BLOCK E — Composition & Flow Variables
- **Compositional Type:** Espiral orgânica (organic spiral ripple composition).
- **Dominant Flow:** Centrípeto (inward-guiding flow focusing on the gliding pair of swans).
- **Negative Space Allocation:** Approximately 25-30% pure white space in calm water highlights and open intervals.

### BLOCK F & G — Density, Complexity & Coloring Experience
- **Complexity Level:** Level 3 (Intermediate adult complexity).
- **Line Density:** Média (balanced medium line density).
- **Coloring Cells:** Distinct black outlines for the swan bodies, paired with smooth, curving water wake cells designed for harmonious coloring.
- **Line Finish:** Pure 100% black line art, zero gray lines, zero grayscale shading, zero gradients, zero solid black fill.

### BLOCK H — Negative Constraints
*Referência:* `art/prompts/NEGATIVE_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Proibições ativas:* No text, watermarks, signatures. No character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, cute/kawaii aesthetic. No fairy tale ugly duckling cliches, cartoon hearts, crowns. No photorealism, 3D render. No rigid geometric mandalas.

## 4. EXECUTABLE SPECIFIC PROMPT (Prompt Final em Inglês)

```text
Contemplative Figurative adult coloring book page, Level 3 intermediate complexity, crisp black line art on pure white paper. Two graceful adult swans gliding serenely across calm lake water, sending gentle spiral ripples through the surface, elevated three-quarter view. Fluid organic neurographic lines flow from the swans' arched necks and wing plumage into expanding watery wakes and rhythmic ripples. Organic spiral composition with centripetal focus, preserving 25-30% clean white negative space in smooth water highlights. Crisp black contours defining the elegant swans, complemented by comfortable, closed water ripple cells for flowing coloring. Clean 100% black lines, no gray, no shading, no gradients, no solid black fills. --no text, watermarks, signatures, character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, kawaii, cartoon hearts, crowns, fairy tale, photorealism, 3D, mandalas, mechanical symmetry.
```
