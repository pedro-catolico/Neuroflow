## 3. PROMPT ARCHITECTURE COMPONENTS (Prompt Architecture v0.1)

### BLOCK A — Identity Constants
*Referência:* `art/prompts/MASTER_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Direção:* Contemplative Figurative identity. Crisp black line art on pure white paper.

### BLOCK B & C — Subject & Structural Setup
- **Subject Description:** A solitary graceful heron lifting off in serene, weightless flight above a mirror-calm lake at daybreak.
- **Framing & Camera Perspective:** Minimalist composition with active perimeter margins, positioning the poised heron in elegant diagonal ascent.
- **Pose / Attitude:** Effortless, dignified wing extension capturing solitary peaceful flight and profound stillness.

### BLOCK D — Neurographic Integration
- **Line Origin:** Minimalist contour lines originate from wingtips and trailing slender legs.
- **Spatial Connection:** A few essential, sweeping curves link the heron's silhouette to subtle water ripples along the lower active border, leaving the vast center pristine.

### BLOCK E — Composition & Flow Variables
- **Compositional Type:** Bordas ativas (active borders with open, empty meditative center).
- **Dominant Flow:** Centrípeto suave (gentle inward visual focus toward the solitary bird).
- **Negative Space Allocation:** Expansive 70-75% pure white negative space celebrating visual silence and meditative pause.

### BLOCK F & G — Density, Complexity & Coloring Experience
- **Complexity Level:** Level 1 (Introductory adult complexity).
- **Line Density:** Muito baixa (very low density, zen minimalism).
- **Coloring Cells:** Clean, graceful open cells in the heron's wings and body, with minimal water ripples, making coloring an act of unhurried meditation.
- **Line Finish:** Pure 100% black line art, zero gray lines, zero grayscale shading, zero gradients, zero solid black fill.

### BLOCK H — Negative Constraints
*Referência:* `art/prompts/NEGATIVE_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Proibições ativas:* No text, watermarks, signatures. No character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, cute/kawaii aesthetic. No photorealism, 3D render. No rigid geometric mandalas or mechanical symmetry. No busy shorelines, no cluttered skies.

## 4. EXECUTABLE SPECIFIC PROMPT (Prompt Final em Inglês)

```text
Contemplative Figurative adult coloring book page, minimalist Level 1 complexity, crisp black line art on pure white paper. A solitary elegant heron taking flight with outstretched wings above a calm lake at dawn, active border framing. Minimalist, essential neurographic lines link wingtips and water ripples along the lower margin, leaving the expansive center light and serene. Active border composition with centripetal focus, preserving 70-75% intentional pure white negative space. Graceful, confident black lines defining the bird's silhouette and a few subtle water ripples, forming spacious, comfortable coloring areas. Clean 100% black lines, no gray, no shading, no gradients, no solid black fills. --no text, watermarks, signatures, character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, kawaii, photorealism, 3D, mandalas, mechanical symmetry, clutter.
```
