## 3. PROMPT ARCHITECTURE COMPONENTS (Prompt Architecture v0.1)

### BLOCK A — Identity Constants
*Referência:* `art/prompts/MASTER_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Direção:* Contemplative Figurative identity. Crisp black line art on pure white paper.

### BLOCK B & C — Subject & Structural Setup
- **Subject Description:** A serene adult human female seated in natural scale and quiet meditation on the pebble shore of a tranquil mountain lake, peacefully surrounded by blooming sacred lotus flowers and floating aquatic leaves emerging from the clear water in natural scale.
- **Framing & Camera Perspective:** Centered medium shot at shore water-level, capturing the dignified seated female meditator with surrounding shoreline, blooming lotuses, and reflective water currents.
- **Pose / Attitude:** Still, grounded meditative sitting posture conveying deep wholeness, mindful integration, and peaceful dignity, completely avoiding New Age miniature-figure or fantasy-goddess cliches.

### BLOCK D — Neurographic Integration
- **Line Origin:** Organic lines originate from the shore stones, water ripples, lotus stems, unfolding petals, and the gentle drape of the figure's garments.
- **Spatial Connection:** Continuous sweeping neurographic arcs flow outward from the meditating figure's posture into the lake surface and surrounding flora, seamlessly uniting human consciousness and natural environment in an unbroken visual rhythm.

### BLOCK E — Composition & Flow Variables
- **Compositional Type:** Expansão central (central focal expansion with balanced aquatic perimeter).
- **Dominant Flow:** Radial / centrífugo (organic radial energy rippling outward from the serene figure into water currents and botanical blooms).
- **Negative Space Allocation:** Approximately 25% pure white negative space preserved in open water reflections and luminous upper atmosphere.

### BLOCK F & G — Density, Complexity & Coloring Experience
- **Complexity Level:** Level 4 (Advanced adult complexity).
- **Line Density:** Média-Alta (medium-high line density with rich, crystalline linear definition).
- **Coloring Cells:** Clear line weight hierarchy: bold defining silhouettes for the meditating female figure and primary lotus blooms, paired with refined, comfortable closed cells in petal layers, water ripples, and shoreline pebbles.
- **Line Finish:** Pure 100% black line art, zero gray lines, zero grayscale shading, zero gradients, zero solid black fill.

### BLOCK H — Negative Constraints
*Referência:* `art/prompts/NEGATIVE_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Proibições ativas:* No text, watermarks, signatures. No character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, cute/kawaii aesthetic. No miniature fairy, tiny woman sitting inside a giant flower petal, fantasy fairy tale, surreal New Age cliche. No photorealism, 3D render, medical diagrams. No rigid geometric mandalas or mechanical symmetry.
