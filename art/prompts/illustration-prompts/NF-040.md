## 3. PROMPT ARCHITECTURE COMPONENTS (Prompt Architecture v0.1)

### BLOCK A — Identity Constants
*Referência:* `art/prompts/MASTER_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Direção:* Contemplative Figurative identity. Crisp black line art on pure white paper.

### BLOCK B & C — Subject & Structural Setup
- **Subject Description:** A still alpine lake at night reflecting the majestic silhouettes of distant mountains and quiet starry heavens on its glass-like surface.
- **Framing & Camera Perspective:** Wide lateral horizontal landscape perspective balancing mountain peaks above with their tranquil watery reflection below.
- **Pose / Attitude:** Deep nocturnal stillness, serene geological reflection, and cosmic peace.

### BLOCK D — Neurographic Integration
- **Line Origin:** Horizontal curving lines originate from mountain ridgelines, quiet water margins, and gentle surface ripples.
- **Spatial Connection:** The mountain contours mirror into the lake, where gentle undulating ripples weave reflection and nocturnal sky into quiet parallel currents.

### BLOCK E — Composition & Flow Variables
- **Compositional Type:** Fluxo lateral (horizontal lateral flow emphasizing panoramic rest).
- **Dominant Flow:** Ondular (gentle, slow undulating ripples across the lake surface).
- **Negative Space Allocation:** Approximately 35-40% pure white space across the open sky and clear mirrored water lanes.

### BLOCK F & G — Density, Complexity & Coloring Experience
- **Complexity Level:** Level 2 (Accessible adult complexity).
- **Line Density:** Baixa-Média (low-medium line density, calm and uncluttered).
- **Coloring Cells:** Spacious, comfortable coloring cells in mountain slopes and water reflection bands, inviting restful coloring sessions.
- **Line Finish:** Pure 100% black line art, zero gray lines, zero grayscale shading, zero gradients, zero solid black fill.

### BLOCK H — Negative Constraints
*Referência:* `art/prompts/NEGATIVE_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Proibições ativas:* No text, watermarks, signatures. No character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, cute/kawaii aesthetic. No solid black skies, dark fills, inverted colors. No photorealism, 3D render. No rigid geometric mandalas.
