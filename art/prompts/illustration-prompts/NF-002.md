## 3. PROMPT ARCHITECTURE COMPONENTS (Prompt Architecture v0.1)

### BLOCK A — Identity Constants
*Referência:* `art/prompts/MASTER_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Direção:* Contemplative Figurative identity. Crisp black line art on pure white paper.

### BLOCK B & C — Subject & Structural Setup
- **Subject Description:** A blooming sacred lotus flower emerging above still water, with elegant unfolding petals, visible floral receptacle, aquatic stems, and floating lily pads.
- **Framing & Camera Perspective:** Close-to-medium eye-level perspective centered on the blossoming lotus, with watery reflections in the foreground and open atmosphere above.
- **Pose / Attitude:** Poised, serene botanical emergence capturing the graceful transition of natural blossoming.

### BLOCK D — Neurographic Integration
- **Line Origin:** Organic lines originate from the floral receptacle and petal tips, extending outward into gentle water ripples and submerged stem networks.
- **Spatial Connection:** Open curvilinear arcs weave the lotus petals directly into the undulating surface currents of the pond in an unbroken living tapestry, avoiding detached floating motifs.

### BLOCK E — Composition & Flow Variables
- **Compositional Type:** Expansão central (central expansion with balanced focal presence).
- **Dominant Flow:** Radial (organic outward blooming energy emanating from the floral core).
- **Negative Space Allocation:** Approximately 25-30% pure white space in the outer upper margins for meditative breathing room.

### BLOCK F & G — Density, Complexity & Coloring Experience
- **Complexity Level:** Level 3 (Intermediate adult complexity).
- **Line Density:** Média (medium line density).
- **Coloring Cells:** Clearly delineated petal contours and water ripples providing diverse cell dimensions: broader petal sections paired with refined interior vein lines.
- **Line Finish:** Pure 100% black line art, zero gray lines, zero grayscale shading, zero gradients, zero solid black fill.

### BLOCK H — Negative Constraints
*Referência:* `art/prompts/NEGATIVE_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Proibições ativas:* No text, watermarks, signatures. No character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, cute/kawaii aesthetic. No photorealism, 3D render, medical diagrams. No rigid geometric mandalas, mechanical kaleidoscope symmetry, or compass-drawn circular patterns.

## 4. EXECUTABLE SPECIFIC PROMPT (Prompt Final em Inglês)

```text
Contemplative Figurative adult coloring book page, Level 3 intermediate complexity, crisp black line art on pure white background. A blooming sacred lotus flower gracefully opening above quiet water, eye-level centered perspective. Fluid neurographic lines emerge from the flower center and petal tips, radiating outward into sweeping curvilinear arcs that structure water ripples and aquatic stems. Central expansion composition with organic radial flow, preserving 25-30% serene white negative space in the upper outer areas. Prominent black structural outlines on the blossom with delicate secondary lines in petals and ripples, creating comfortable enclosed coloring cells. Clean 100% black lines, no gray, no shading, no gradients, no solid black fills. --no text, watermarks, signatures, character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, kawaii, photorealism, 3D, rigid mandalas, mechanical symmetry, compass circles.
```
