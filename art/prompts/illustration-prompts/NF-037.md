## 3. PROMPT ARCHITECTURE COMPONENTS (Prompt Architecture v0.1)

### BLOCK A — Identity Constants
*Referência:* `art/prompts/MASTER_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Direção:* Contemplative Figurative identity. Crisp black line art on pure white paper.

### BLOCK B & C — Subject & Structural Setup
- **Subject Description:** A blooming Brazilian trumpet tree (ipê) in peak flowering splendor, with an explosion of trumpet blossoms and a cloud of detached petals floating in the breeze.
- **Framing & Camera Perspective:** Upward medium view centered on the spreading flower-laden canopy and floating petal constellation.
- **Pose / Attitude:** Vibrant, ephemeral floral burst celebrating seasonal transformation and poetic impermanence.

### BLOCK D — Neurographic Integration
- **Line Origin:** Curving lines originate from the sculpted branch elbows, floral petals, and floating blossoms.
- **Spatial Connection:** The dense floral canopy dissolves naturally into floating petal swarms and gentle air eddies, forming a biological floral nebula.

### BLOCK E — Composition & Flow Variables
- **Compositional Type:** Campo denso (dense field of blossoms and drifting petals).
- **Dominant Flow:** Radial (blooming radial expansion drifting into breeze).
- **Negative Space Allocation:** Approximately 20-25% pure white negative space in the atmospheric sky openings between petal clusters.

### BLOCK F & G — Density, Complexity & Coloring Experience
- **Complexity Level:** Level 4 (Advanced adult complexity).
- **Line Density:** Média-Alta (medium-high line density with exquisite botanical clarity).
- **Coloring Cells:** Richly articulated individual blossoms, curving branch knots, and drifting petal cells comfortably sized for rich color blending.
- **Line Finish:** Pure 100% black line art, zero gray lines, zero grayscale shading, zero gradients, zero solid black fill.

### BLOCK H — Negative Constraints
*Referência:* `art/prompts/NEGATIVE_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Proibições ativas:* No text, watermarks, signatures. No character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, cute/kawaii aesthetic. No space galaxy sci-fi stars, cosmic spaceships, fantasy glitter. No photorealism, 3D render. No rigid geometric mandalas.

## 4. EXECUTABLE SPECIFIC PROMPT (Prompt Final em Inglês)

```text
Contemplative Figurative adult coloring book page, Level 4 advanced complexity, crisp black line art on pure white paper. A magnificent blooming ipê tree canopy overflowing with trumpet flowers, with a cloud of detached petals floating weightlessly on the spring breeze, upward framing. Fluid organic neurographic lines weave the twisted branches, dense blossoms, and floating petal swarms into an organic biological nebula. Dense field composition with blooming radial flow, preserving 20-25% clean white negative space in luminous sky intervals. Firm black branch outlines framing intricate, comfortable floral and petal cells for an immersive coloring experience. Clean 100% black lines, no gray, no shading, no gradients, no solid black fills. --no text, watermarks, signatures, character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, kawaii, sci-fi galaxy, spaceships, glitter, photorealism, 3D, mandalas, mechanical symmetry.
```
