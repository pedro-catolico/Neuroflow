## 3. PROMPT ARCHITECTURE COMPONENTS (Prompt Architecture v0.1)

### BLOCK A — Identity Constants
*Referência:* `art/prompts/MASTER_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Direção:* Contemplative Figurative identity. Crisp black line art on pure white paper.

### BLOCK B & C — Subject & Structural Setup
- **Subject Description:** A serene profile of an adult human female face with closed eyes, gently tilted upward as she feels the soft breeze upon her cheek.
- **Framing & Camera Perspective:** Close-up profile framing with active edges along the jaw and hair, leaving the surrounding canvas open and weightless.
- **Pose / Attitude:** Still, intimate, peaceful mindfulness embracing the present instant with deep relaxation.

### BLOCK D — Neurographic Integration
- **Line Origin:** Minimalist curving lines originate from the eyelid, calm lips, jawline, and flowing hair strands.
- **Spatial Connection:** A few graceful hair strands and subtle wind contours trace into the active margins, anchoring the portrait without crowding the open space.

### BLOCK E — Composition & Flow Variables
- **Compositional Type:** Bordas ativas (active border framing focused on a minimalist intimate portrait).
- **Dominant Flow:** Centrípeto / ondulante (inward centripetal rest with gentle undulating breeze curves).
- **Negative Space Allocation:** Generous 60-70% pure white negative space celebrating quietude and breathing room.

### BLOCK F & G — Density, Complexity & Coloring Experience
- **Complexity Level:** Level 1 (Introductory adult complexity).
- **Line Density:** Muito baixa (very low density, zen minimalism).
- **Coloring Cells:** Large, pure, welcoming open cells in the serene facial profile, neck, and smooth hair sections, creating an effortless, deeply relaxing coloring experience.
- **Line Finish:** Pure 100% black line art, zero gray lines, zero grayscale shading, zero gradients, zero solid black fill.

### BLOCK H — Negative Constraints
*Referência:* `art/prompts/NEGATIVE_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Proibições ativas:* No text, watermarks, signatures. No character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, cute/kawaii aesthetic. No glamorous fashion makeup, exaggerated eyelashes, commercial beauty illustration. No photorealism, 3D render. No rigid geometric mandalas.

Contemplative Figurative adult coloring book page, minimalist Level 1 complexity, crisp black line art on pure white paper. A serene profile portrait of an adult female face with closed eyes feeling a gentle breeze, intimate close-up profile framing. Active border composition with gentle undulating flow, preserving 60-70% intentional white negative space.
