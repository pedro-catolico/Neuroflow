## 3. PROMPT ARCHITECTURE COMPONENTS (Prompt Architecture v0.1)

### BLOCK A — Identity Constants
*Referência:* `art/prompts/MASTER_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Direção:* Contemplative Figurative identity. Crisp black line art on pure white paper.

### BLOCK B & C — Subject & Structural Setup
- **Subject Description:** An expansive, sunlit field of flourishing sunflowers in diverse perspectives, featuring prominent foreground sunflower heads revealing biological seed spirals, radiating golden petals, textured stems, and broad leaves extending to the rolling horizon.
- **Framing & Camera Perspective:** Low-angle wide landscape perspective, with close-up detailed sunflower heads in the foreground and a rhythmic rolling field receding naturally toward the distant skyline.
- **Pose / Attitude:** Vibrant, serene botanical vitality celebrating joyful natural growth and the rhythmic abundance of nature.

### BLOCK D — Neurographic Integration
- **Line Origin:** Organic lines originate from the natural biological spiral seed patterns in the flower heads and the arching ribs of the sunflower petals and leaves.
- **Spatial Connection:** The natural botanical spirals transition into continuous flowing neurographic contour lines that connect adjacent blossoms, stalks, and warm atmospheric breeze lines in an unbroken living field.

### BLOCK E — Composition & Flow Variables
- **Compositional Type:** Campo denso (dense field composition rich in organic texture across the entire lower and middle canvas).
- **Dominant Flow:** Radial múltiplo (multiple organic radial focal centers blossoming across the sunflower field).
- **Negative Space Allocation:** Approximately 20-25% intentional open white space preserved in the sunlit upper sky band and open intervals between foreground flower heads.

### BLOCK F & G — Density, Complexity & Coloring Experience
- **Complexity Level:** Level 3 (Intermediate adult complexity).
- **Line Density:** Média-Alta (medium-high line density balanced by generous petal shapes).
- **Coloring Cells:** Clearly delineated, comfortable coloring cells with distinct line weight hierarchy: bold primary silhouettes for main floral heads and petals, paired with medium stem contours and fine interior seed spirals, avoiding muddy clutter.
- **Line Finish:** Pure 100% black line art, zero gray lines, zero grayscale shading, zero gradients, zero solid black fill.

### BLOCK H — Negative Constraints
*Referência:* `art/prompts/NEGATIVE_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Proibições ativas:* No text, watermarks, signatures. No character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, cute/kawaii aesthetic. No sacred geometry symbols, esoteric mystic signs, religious mandalas, mechanical kaleidoscope symmetry. No photorealism, 3D render.

## 4. EXECUTABLE SPECIFIC PROMPT (Prompt Final em Inglês)

```text
Contemplative Figurative adult coloring book page, Level 3 intermediate complexity, crisp black line art on pure white paper. An expansive sunlit field of thriving sunflowers, with large foreground blossoms revealing natural biological seed spirals, textured stems, and sweeping petals, low-angle landscape framing. Fluid organic neurographic lines weave the natural seed spirals and petal contours into an unbroken botanical rhythm connecting flowers, foliage, and breeze. Dense field composition with multiple organic radial focal points, preserving 20-25% clean white negative space across the upper sky and petal intervals. Strong black outlines defining the floral heads and stems, harmonized with comfortable enclosed petal and leaf cells for relaxed coloring. Clean 100% black lines, no gray, no shading, no gradients, no solid black fills. --no text, watermarks, signatures, character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, kawaii, sacred geometry, esoteric symbols, mystic mandalas, mechanical symmetry, photorealism, 3D.
```
