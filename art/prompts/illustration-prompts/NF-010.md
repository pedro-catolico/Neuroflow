## 3. PROMPT ARCHITECTURE COMPONENTS (Prompt Architecture v0.1)

### BLOCK A — Identity Constants
*Referência:* `art/prompts/MASTER_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Direção:* Contemplative Figurative identity. Crisp black line art on pure white paper.

### BLOCK B & C — Subject & Structural Setup
- **Subject Description:** A tranquil zen garden sanctuary featuring a weathered wooden timber gateway (torii/portal), smooth stepping stones meandering through moss, and lush bamboo foliage.
- **Framing & Camera Perspective:** Eye-level welcoming perspective looking through the rustic wooden gate along the winding garden path.
- **Pose / Attitude:** Still, sheltered environmental refuge inviting mindful walking and quiet contemplation.

### BLOCK D — Neurographic Integration
- **Line Origin:** Curvilinear lines originate from the timber woodgrain of the gateway, smooth stepping-stone edges, and slender bamboo shoots.
- **Spatial Connection:** The architectural lines of the wooden frame dissolve seamlessly into surrounding leaves, creeping mosses, and winding garden paths in continuous contours.

### BLOCK E — Composition & Flow Variables
- **Compositional Type:** Campo denso (dense garden landscape field).
- **Dominant Flow:** Ondular múltiplo (multiple undulating meandering flow paths leading through the landscape).
- **Negative Space Allocation:** Approximately 25-30% pure white negative space preserved in open path spaces and tranquil sky intervals.

### BLOCK F & G — Density, Complexity & Coloring Experience
- **Complexity Level:** Level 3 (Intermediate adult complexity).
- **Line Density:** Média (medium balanced line density).
- **Coloring Cells:** Firm black outlines for the wooden gate and stone path, balanced with comfortable closed foliage cells for foliage, bamboo, and gravel.
- **Line Finish:** Pure 100% black line art, zero gray lines, zero grayscale shading, zero gradients, zero solid black fill.

### BLOCK H — Negative Constraints
*Referência:* `art/prompts/NEGATIVE_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Proibições ativas:* No text, watermarks, signatures. No character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, cute/kawaii aesthetic. No photorealism, 3D render. No fantasy fairy tale temples, no rigid geometric mandalas or mechanical symmetry.

## 4. EXECUTABLE SPECIFIC PROMPT (Prompt Final em Inglês)

```text
Contemplative Figurative adult coloring book page, Level 3 intermediate complexity, crisp black line art on pure white paper. A meditative garden refuge with a rustic wooden portal gate and curved stone path meandering through bamboo and moss, eye-level framing. Organic neurographic lines flow from the timber woodgrain and stone edges, merging architecture and lush foliage into continuous harmonic contours. Dense field composition with undulating multi-stream flow, preserving 25-30% clean white negative space along open path stones and garden air. Definite black structural outlines on the wooden portal, harmonized with comfortable enclosed foliage and gravel coloring cells. Clean 100% black lines, no gray, no shading, no gradients, no solid black fills. --no text, watermarks, signatures, character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, kawaii, fantasy temple, photorealism, 3D, mandalas, mechanical symmetry.
```
