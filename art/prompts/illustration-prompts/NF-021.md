## 3. PROMPT ARCHITECTURE COMPONENTS (Prompt Architecture v0.1)

### BLOCK A — Identity Constants
*Referência:* `art/prompts/MASTER_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Direção:* Contemplative Figurative identity. Crisp black line art on pure white paper.

### BLOCK B & C — Subject & Structural Setup
- **Subject Description:** A flock of graceful seabirds soaring in effortless formation high above a distant chain of rugged oceanic islands.
- **Framing & Camera Perspective:** High-angle expansive coastal view establishing dramatic scale contrast between soaring birds in the sky and miniature islands below.
- **Pose / Attitude:** Gliding flight posture with wings outstretched, capturing pure aerodynamic lightness and freedom.

### BLOCK D — Neurographic Integration
- **Line Origin:** Curving lines originate from the tips of bird wings and the coastlines of the islands below.
- **Spatial Connection:** Broad sweeping atmospheric thermals weave through the flock, linking the flight paths directly to the ocean swells and island contours.

### BLOCK E — Composition & Flow Variables
- **Compositional Type:** Contraste de escala (scale contrast between sweeping airborne flock and distant island landforms).
- **Dominant Flow:** Radial distribuído (distributed radial flow opening outward with oceanic thermals).
- **Negative Space Allocation:** Approximately 35-40% pure white negative space celebrating vast sky and oceanic breadth.

### BLOCK F & G — Density, Complexity & Coloring Experience
- **Complexity Level:** Level 2 (Accessible adult complexity).
- **Line Density:** Baixa-Média (low-medium line density).
- **Coloring Cells:** Clear silhouettes of seabirds and distinct island coastlines surrounded by comfortable, flowing thermal currents, perfect for smooth coloring.
- **Line Finish:** Pure 100% black line art, zero gray lines, zero grayscale shading, zero gradients, zero solid black fill.

### BLOCK H — Negative Constraints
*Referência:* `art/prompts/NEGATIVE_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Proibições ativas:* No text, watermarks, signatures. No character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, cute/kawaii aesthetic. No photorealism, 3D render. No rigid geometric mandalas or mechanical symmetry. No airplanes, no boats, no cityscapes.

## 4. EXECUTABLE SPECIFIC PROMPT (Prompt Final em Inglês)

```text
Contemplative Figurative adult coloring book page, Level 2 complexity, crisp black line art on pure white paper. A flock of wild seabirds gliding freely in the sky high above distant rocky oceanic islands, high-angle panoramic framing. Fluid organic neurographic air currents weave between the soaring birds and trace the coastline below, connecting sky and sea in graceful harmony. Scale contrast composition with distributed radial flow, preserving 35-40% luminous white negative space in the expansive sky. Distinct black outlines for the gliding birds and island landforms, paired with smooth thermal wave cells for relaxed coloring. Clean 100% black lines, no gray, no shading, no gradients, no solid black fills. --no text, watermarks, signatures, character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, kawaii, airplanes, boats, photorealism, 3D, mandalas, mechanical symmetry.
```
