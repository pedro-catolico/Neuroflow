## 3. PROMPT ARCHITECTURE COMPONENTS (Prompt Architecture v0.1)

### BLOCK A — Identity Constants
*Referência:* `art/prompts/MASTER_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Direção:* Contemplative Figurative identity. Crisp black line art on pure white paper.

### BLOCK B & C — Subject & Structural Setup
- **Subject Description:** A zen kinetic arrangement of smooth suspended balancing stones and a weighted metallic pendulum poised in mid-air.
- **Framing & Camera Perspective:** Intimate eye-level focal framing capturing the suspended stones, pendulum cord, and surrounding invisible energy fields made visible.
- **Pose / Attitude:** Suspended equilibrium, motionless balance holding subtle dynamic tension.

### BLOCK D — Neurographic Integration
- **Line Origin:** Curving force lines emanate from the stone apexes, the pendulum tip, and suspension cords.
- **Spatial Connection:** Flowing organic orbital curves radiate and loop between the suspended stones, weaving dynamic lines of gravitational and magnetic tension throughout the space.

### BLOCK E — Composition & Flow Variables
- **Compositional Type:** Campo denso (dense field composition rich in organic linear energy).
- **Dominant Flow:** Radial múltiplo (multiple organic radial force fields interacting harmoniously).
- **Negative Space Allocation:** Approximately 25% pure white space maintained in rhythmic intervals between magnetic line clusters.

### BLOCK F & G — Density, Complexity & Coloring Experience
- **Complexity Level:** Level 4 (Advanced adult complexity).
- **Line Density:** Média-Alta (medium-high line density).
- **Coloring Cells:** Firm structural contours defining each stone and pendulum, accompanied by rich, rhythmic force field bands forming comfortable closed coloring regions.
- **Line Finish:** Pure 100% black line art, zero gray lines, zero grayscale shading, zero gradients, zero solid black fill.

### BLOCK H — Negative Constraints
*Referência:* `art/prompts/NEGATIVE_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Proibições ativas:* No text, watermarks, signatures. No character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, cute/kawaii aesthetic. No technical physics blueprints, circuit schematics, clinical laboratory apparatus. No rigid geometric mandalas or ruler-straight mechanical lines.
