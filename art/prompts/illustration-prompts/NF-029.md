## 3. PROMPT ARCHITECTURE COMPONENTS (Prompt Architecture v0.1)

### BLOCK A — Identity Constants
*Referência:* `art/prompts/MASTER_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Direção:* Contemplative Figurative identity. Crisp black line art on pure white paper.

### BLOCK B & C — Subject & Structural Setup
- **Subject Description:** A harmonious symbolic constellation of fluid organic geometric forms (pebble-like ovals, curvilinear petals, and resonant rings) connected in a balanced network.
- **Framing & Camera Perspective:** Decentralized full-page panoramic framing presenting a balanced constellation of interconnected organic nodes.
- **Pose / Attitude:** Suspended equilibrium, intellectual clarity, and meditative interconnectedness.

### BLOCK D — Neurographic Integration
- **Line Origin:** Curving lines originate from the perimeter walls of rounded organic structural nodes.
- **Spatial Connection:** Flowing neurographic bridge lines connect nodes across varied distances, creating a rhythmic, continuous circulatory network across the whole plane.

### BLOCK E — Composition & Flow Variables
- **Compositional Type:** Rede distribuída (distributed network composition across multiple visual focal points).
- **Dominant Flow:** Radial de múltiplos focos (multi-focal radial flow radiating from interconnected nodes).
- **Negative Space Allocation:** Approximately 25% pure white space preserved in open channels and tranquil background intervals.

### BLOCK F & G — Density, Complexity & Coloring Experience
- **Complexity Level:** Level 4 (Advanced adult complexity).
- **Line Density:** Média-Alta (medium-high line density).
- **Coloring Cells:** Bold black outlines defining major organic nodes, with fine interior concentric waves and bridge lines creating inviting, varied coloring paths.
- **Line Finish:** Pure 100% black line art, zero gray lines, zero grayscale shading, zero gradients, zero solid black fill.

### BLOCK H — Negative Constraints
*Referência:* `art/prompts/NEGATIVE_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Proibições ativas:* No text, watermarks, signatures. No character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, cute/kawaii aesthetic. No rigid technical blueprints, circuit diagrams, geometric mechanical mandalas, compass circles. No photorealism, 3D render.
