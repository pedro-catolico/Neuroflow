## 3. PROMPT ARCHITECTURE COMPONENTS (Prompt Architecture v0.1)

### BLOCK A — Identity Constants
*Referência:* `art/prompts/MASTER_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Direção:* Contemplative Figurative identity. Crisp black line art on pure white paper.

### BLOCK B & C — Subject & Structural Setup
- **Subject Description:** An intimate botanical-geological study of ancient lakeside boulders weathered by time, adorned with growing colonies of textured mosses and spreading lichen crusts.
- **Framing & Camera Perspective:** Close-up eye-level framing focused on the textured stone surfaces, velvety moss cushions, and still lake water edge.
- **Pose / Attitude:** Still, patient, eternal presence celebrating slow geological and botanical time.

### BLOCK D — Neurographic Integration
- **Line Origin:** Curving contour lines emerge from rock fractures, boulder rims, and expanding lichen rosettes.
- **Spatial Connection:** The spreading lichen outlines flow smoothly into the organic cracks of the rocks and merge with the gentle water lap at the base.

### BLOCK E — Composition & Flow Variables
- **Compositional Type:** Campo denso (dense field composition rich in organic texture).
- **Dominant Flow:** Ondular (undulating wave-like current of lichen colonies and rock contours).
- **Negative Space Allocation:** Approximately 20-25% pure white negative space in open rock face highlights and still lake water.

### BLOCK F & G — Density, Complexity & Coloring Experience
- **Complexity Level:** Level 3 (Intermediate adult complexity).
- **Line Density:** Média-Alta (medium-high line density composed of clean, crisp line art).
- **Coloring Cells:** Distinct rock boundary contours enclosing varied lichen cushions and pebble clusters, creating rewarding cells for layered coloring.
- **Line Finish:** Pure 100% black line art, zero gray lines, zero grayscale shading, zero gradients, zero solid black fill.

### BLOCK H — Negative Constraints
*Referência:* `art/prompts/NEGATIVE_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Proibições ativas:* No text, watermarks, signatures. No character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, cute/kawaii aesthetic. No photorealism, 3D render. No rigid geometric mandalas or mechanical symmetry. No messy unprintable mud.

## 4. EXECUTABLE SPECIFIC PROMPT (Prompt Final em Inglês)

```text
Contemplative Figurative adult coloring book page, Level 3 intermediate complexity, crisp black line art on pure white paper. An intimate shoreline study of ancient river boulders covered in expanding lichen patterns and velvety mosses by still water, close-up framing. Fluid organic neurographic lines trace rock fractures and spreading lichen rosettes, weaving stone, plant, and water into continuous rhythmic contours. Dense field composition with undulating organic flow, preserving 20-25% clean white negative space in smooth rock facets and calm water reflections. Firm black contours defining the stone boulders, harmonized with comfortable enclosed botanical cells for relaxing coloring. Clean 100% black lines, no gray, no shading, no gradients, no solid black fills. --no text, watermarks, signatures, character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, kawaii, photorealism, 3D, mandalas, mechanical symmetry, muddy clutter.
```
