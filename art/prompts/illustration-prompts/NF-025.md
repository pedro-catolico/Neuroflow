## 3. PROMPT ARCHITECTURE COMPONENTS (Prompt Architecture v0.1)

### BLOCK A — Identity Constants
*Referência:* `art/prompts/MASTER_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Direção:* Contemplative Figurative identity. Crisp black line art on pure white paper.

### BLOCK B & C — Subject & Structural Setup
- **Subject Description:** A dignified adult male figure standing quietly on a shore beach at twilight, watching the full moon rise gracefully above the oceanic horizon.
- **Framing & Camera Perspective:** Full-body vertical landscape shot, with the standing male silhouette grounded on the shore as the moon ascends above.
- **Pose / Attitude:** Erect, calm, contemplative posture of spiritual elevation and upward gazing.

### BLOCK D — Neurographic Integration
- **Line Origin:** Curving lines emerge from sea ripples at his feet, tidal shore markings, and his upright silhouette.
- **Spatial Connection:** Luminous reflective water lines flow vertically upward toward the rising moon, weaving ocean, human watcher, and night sky together.

### BLOCK E — Composition & Flow Variables
- **Compositional Type:** Diagonal dinâmica vertical (vertical dynamic diagonal rising steadily from shore to sky).
- **Dominant Flow:** Ascendente (upward rising flow drawing the eye from the earth to celestial heights).
- **Negative Space Allocation:** Approximately 30-35% pure white negative space surrounding the rising moon and sky expanse.

### BLOCK F & G — Density, Complexity & Coloring Experience
- **Complexity Level:** Level 2 (Accessible adult complexity).
- **Line Density:** Baixa na praia (low at base), aumentando suavemente para densidade média nas correntes celestes superiores.
- **Coloring Cells:** Clean, graceful outlines defining the human figure, tidal curves, and lunar orb, providing open and comfortable coloring spaces.
- **Line Finish:** Pure 100% black line art, zero gray lines, zero grayscale shading, zero gradients, zero solid black fill.

### BLOCK H — Negative Constraints
*Referência:* `art/prompts/NEGATIVE_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Proibições ativas:* No text, watermarks, signatures. No character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, cute/kawaii aesthetic. No hero pose, fantasy magic beams, sci-fi aura. No photorealism, 3D render. No rigid geometric mandalas.

## 4. EXECUTABLE SPECIFIC PROMPT (Prompt Final em Inglês)

```text
Contemplative Figurative adult coloring book page, Level 2 complexity, crisp black line art on pure white paper. A serene adult male figure standing quietly on the shore of a calm sea watching the full moon rise over the horizon, vertical full-body framing. Vertical dynamic composition with ascending flow, preserving 30-35% clean white negative space around the upper sky and moon. Crisp black contours defining the contemplative male silhouette, accompanied by open, comfortable water and sky cells for effortless coloring.
```
