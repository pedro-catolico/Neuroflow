## 3. PROMPT ARCHITECTURE COMPONENTS (Prompt Architecture v0.1)

### BLOCK A — Identity Constants
*Referência:* `art/prompts/MASTER_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Direção:* Contemplative Figurative identity. Crisp black line art on pure white paper.

### BLOCK B & C — Subject & Structural Setup
- **Subject Description:** A monumental millennial olive tree with an ancient, gnarled, twisting trunk and arching branches ascending into an expansive organic spiral against the sky.
- **Framing & Camera Perspective:** Dramatic low-angle upward perspective (contra-plongée) looking up from the base along the spiraling trunk into the canopy.
- **Pose / Attitude:** Majestic, enduring botanical power twisting skyward with timeless resilience.

### BLOCK D — Neurographic Integration
- **Line Origin:** Curving contour lines emerge from the deep fissures and twisting muscle-like wood of the ancient trunk.
- **Spatial Connection:** The twisted grain of the olive wood flows continuously into the ascending branch spirals, unfurling delicate leaf clusters across the sky in a cosmic botanical spiral.

### BLOCK E — Composition & Flow Variables
- **Compositional Type:** Espiral orgânica (organic spiral composition unfolding vertically from base to sky).
- **Dominant Flow:** Espiral (ascending spiral flow guiding the eye upward with majestic power).
- **Negative Space Allocation:** Approximately 20-25% clean white negative space preserved in rhythmic sky openings between outer spiraling branch tips.

### BLOCK F & G — Density, Complexity & Coloring Experience
- **Complexity Level:** Level 5 (Master adult complexity).
- **Line Density:** Média a Alta (medium-high to high density of rich twisting bark and foliage).
- **Coloring Cells:** Hundreds of clearly articulated, comfortably closed coloring cells: powerful primary trunk bands, medium branch contours, and fine olive leaves. Zero unprintable micro-scrawl.
- **Line Finish:** Pure 100% black line art, zero gray lines, zero grayscale shading, zero gradients, zero solid black fill.

### BLOCK H — Negative Constraints
*Referência:* `art/prompts/NEGATIVE_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Proibições ativas:* No text, watermarks, signatures. No character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, cute/kawaii aesthetic. No mythical tree faces, dryads, fantasy creatures. No photorealism, 3D render. No rigid geometric mandalas or mechanical symmetry.

Contemplative Figurative adult coloring book page, Level 5 master complexity, crisp black line art on pure white paper.
