## 3. PROMPT ARCHITECTURE COMPONENTS (Prompt Architecture v0.1)

### BLOCK A — Identity Constants
*Referência:* `art/prompts/MASTER_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Direção:* Contemplative Figurative identity. Crisp black line art on pure white paper.

### BLOCK B & C — Subject & Structural Setup
- **Subject Description:** An ancient curved stone staircase winding rhythmically up a flowering hillside, bordered by climbing wild roses and an open wrought-iron gate.
- **Framing & Camera Perspective:** Ascending three-quarter perspective following the meandering curve of the stone steps through the lush hillside.
- **Pose / Attitude:** Enduring, welcoming architectural rhythm inviting quiet step-by-step progress.

### BLOCK D — Neurographic Integration
- **Line Origin:** Curving lines originate from worn stone step edges, wrought-iron scrolls, and creeping floral vines.
- **Spatial Connection:** The rhythmic treads of the staircase flow continuously into adjacent rock walls and flowering bushes, weaving built craft and nature into an unbroken journey.

### BLOCK E — Composition & Flow Variables
- **Compositional Type:** Fluxo lateral (horizontal-diagonal winding flow traversing the hillside).
- **Dominant Flow:** Ondular (undulating rhythmic stair treads guiding the viewer's gaze upward).
- **Negative Space Allocation:** Approximately 25-30% clean white space preserved in open stair tread highlights and sky intervals.

### BLOCK F & G — Density, Complexity & Coloring Experience
- **Complexity Level:** Level 3 (Intermediate adult complexity).
- **Line Density:** Média (balanced medium line density).
- **Coloring Cells:** Distinct stone stair shapes and iron scrollwork paired with generous petal and leaf clusters, creating rhythmic and comfortable coloring spaces.
- **Line Finish:** Pure 100% black line art, zero gray lines, zero grayscale shading, zero gradients, zero solid black fill.

### BLOCK H — Negative Constraints
*Referência:* `art/prompts/NEGATIVE_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Proibições ativas:* No text, watermarks, signatures. No character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, cute/kawaii aesthetic. No fantasy gothic ruins, haunted houses, medieval castles. No photorealism, 3D render. No rigid geometric mandalas.

## 4. EXECUTABLE SPECIFIC PROMPT (Prompt Final em Inglês)

```text
Contemplative Figurative adult coloring book page, Level 3 intermediate complexity, crisp black line art on pure white paper. An ancient weathered stone staircase winding gracefully up a flowering hillside beside a wrought-iron garden gate, three-quarter ascending perspective. Organic neurographic lines connect the curved step edges and iron scrolls to climbing rose vines and hillside terrain in continuous rhythmic harmony. Lateral composition with undulating winding flow, preserving 25-30% clean white negative space along stone treads and sky. Confident black structural outlines for the steps and gate, balanced by comfortable, closed flower and foliage cells for meditative coloring. Clean 100% black lines, no gray, no shading, no gradients, no solid black fills. --no text, watermarks, signatures, character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, kawaii, gothic ruins, haunted castle, photorealism, 3D, mandalas, mechanical symmetry.
```
