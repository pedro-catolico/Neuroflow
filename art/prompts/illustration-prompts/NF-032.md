## 3. PROMPT ARCHITECTURE COMPONENTS (Prompt Architecture v0.1)

### BLOCK A — Identity Constants
*Referência:* `art/prompts/MASTER_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Direção:* Contemplative Figurative identity. Crisp black line art on pure white paper.

### BLOCK B & C — Subject & Structural Setup
- **Subject Description:** Two adult figures seated quietly side by side on the edge of an elevated coastal cliff, gazing peacefully toward the vast ocean sunset.
- **Framing & Camera Perspective:** Medium-long shot from behind and slightly three-quarter angle, showing their seated posture, the cliff edge, and wide sea horizon.
- **Pose / Attitude:** Still, companionable seated posture sharing silent presence, peaceful intimacy, and mutual contemplation.

### BLOCK D — Neurographic Integration
- **Line Origin:** Curving lines originate from the cliff strata, the folds of their resting cloaks, and the crests of sea waves below.
- **Spatial Connection:** The geological cliff striations and draped garments converge along a graceful diagonal, blending harmoniously into the undulating oceanic swells.

### BLOCK E — Composition & Flow Variables
- **Compositional Type:** Diagonal dinâmica (dynamic diagonal composition linking cliff summit to sea horizon).
- **Dominant Flow:** Ondular (undulating wave flow along the diagonal axis of the cliff and sea).
- **Negative Space Allocation:** Approximately 25-30% pure white space in the luminous sky above the ocean horizon.

### BLOCK F & G — Density, Complexity & Coloring Experience
- **Complexity Level:** Level 4 (Advanced adult complexity).
- **Line Density:** Média (balanced medium density with rich linear depth).
- **Coloring Cells:** Clear black outlines defining the serene couple and cliff edge, enriched with comfortable, flowing waves and clothing cells for deep coloring satisfaction.
- **Line Finish:** Pure 100% black line art, zero gray lines, zero grayscale shading, zero gradients, zero solid black fill.

### BLOCK H — Negative Constraints
*Referência:* `art/prompts/NEGATIVE_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Proibições ativas:* No text, watermarks, signatures. No character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, cute/kawaii aesthetic. No romantic melodrama, kissing tropes, exaggerated comic expressions. No photorealism, 3D render. No rigid geometric mandalas.

## 4. EXECUTABLE SPECIFIC PROMPT (Prompt Final em Inglês)

```text
Contemplative Figurative adult coloring book page, Level 4 advanced complexity, crisp black line art on pure white paper. Two adult human figures seated quietly side by side on a coastal cliff ledge, looking out over the ocean at sunset, three-quarter back view. Fluid organic neurographic lines flow from the cliff strata and draped clothing folds, converging with the undulating ocean waves along a graceful diagonal axis. Dynamic diagonal composition with wave-like flow, preserving 25-30% clean white negative space in the glowing evening sky. Bold black contours for the seated figures and cliff edge, paired with rich, comfortable closed cells in the water and apparel. Clean 100% black lines, no gray, no shading, no gradients, no solid black fills. --no text, watermarks, signatures, character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, kawaii, romantic melodrama, photorealism, 3D, mandalas, mechanical symmetry.
```
