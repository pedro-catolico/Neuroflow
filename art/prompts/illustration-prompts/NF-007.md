## 3. PROMPT ARCHITECTURE COMPONENTS (Prompt Architecture v0.1)

### BLOCK A — Identity Constants
*Referência:* `art/prompts/MASTER_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Direção:* Contemplative Figurative identity. Crisp black line art on pure white paper.

### BLOCK B & C — Subject & Structural Setup
- **Subject Description:** Two serene adult figures walking side by side in quiet contemplation across a vast, open terrain, their forms dignified and graceful.
- **Framing & Camera Perspective:** Full-body medium-long shot in three-quarter profile view, capturing their peaceful stride and elongated ground shadows.
- **Pose / Attitude:** Harmonious walking posture, expressing companionship, silent harmony, and mutual presence without theatrical drama.

### BLOCK D — Neurographic Integration
- **Line Origin:** Flowing contour lines originate from the folds of their draped garments and the elongated shadows cast upon the earth.
- **Spatial Connection:** Two separate streams of rhythmic terrain lines converge gently along an organic diagonal, uniting both figures with the ground and horizon.

### BLOCK E — Composition & Flow Variables
- **Compositional Type:** Diagonal dinâmica (dynamic diagonal composition traversing the page with poetic balance).
- **Dominant Flow:** Ondular convergente (two undulating streams converging smoothly into a single unified current).
- **Negative Space Allocation:** Approximately 30-35% open pure white space preserved in the quiet upper sky and foreground pauses.

### BLOCK F & G — Density, Complexity & Coloring Experience
- **Complexity Level:** Level 2 (Accessible adult complexity).
- **Line Density:** Média-Baixa (medium-low density).
- **Coloring Cells:** Clear primary outlines for both walking figures, paired with medium, undulating terrain bands, creating comfortable, welcoming coloring cells.
- **Line Finish:** Pure 100% black line art, zero gray lines, zero grayscale shading, zero gradients, zero solid black fill.

### BLOCK H — Negative Constraints
*Referência:* `art/prompts/NEGATIVE_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Proibições ativas:* No text, watermarks, signatures. No character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, cute/kawaii aesthetic. No hero poses, comic illustration, dramatic romance tropes. No photorealism, 3D render. No rigid geometric mandalas or mechanical symmetry.
