## 3. PROMPT ARCHITECTURE COMPONENTS (Prompt Architecture v0.1)

### BLOCK A — Identity Constants
*Referência:* `art/prompts/MASTER_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Direção:* Contemplative Figurative identity. Crisp black line art on pure white paper.

### BLOCK B & C — Subject & Structural Setup
- **Subject Description:** A serene adult human male seated quietly on an elevated natural ground, gazing pensively toward the night sky in still contemplation.
- **Framing & Camera Perspective:** Three-quarter side view in medium shot, positioning the seated male figure gracefully in the lower corner/mid-left area, facing outward into expansive space.
- **Pose / Attitude:** Still, noble, introspective seated posture, free from dramatic theatricality, embodying quiet dignity and inner perspective.

### BLOCK D — Neurographic Integration
- **Line Origin:** Organic curving lines emerge seamlessly from the figure's shoulder, garment folds, and line of sight.
- **Spatial Connection:** The lines unfurl outward across the celestial sky in an open, organic centrifugal spiral, weaving the human observer into atmospheric starry waves and cosmic rhythms.

### BLOCK E — Composition & Flow Variables
- **Compositional Type:** Espiral orgânica (organic spiral composition unfolding from the human subject).
- **Dominant Flow:** Espiral centrífugo (centrifugal spiral sweeping out from figure towards page perimeters).
- **Negative Space Allocation:** Approximately 25-30% open white space concentrated near the focal origin and in open rhythmic intervals between spiral bands.

### BLOCK F & G — Density, Complexity & Coloring Experience
- **Complexity Level:** Level 3 (Intermediate adult complexity).
- **Line Density:** Média (medium density centrally, increasing gradually towards outer cosmic margins).
- **Coloring Cells:** Clear line weight hierarchy: bold defining outlines for the masculine silhouette and seated form, paired with graceful medium and fine flowing lines in the cosmic spiral bands, creating comfortable coloring cells.
- **Line Finish:** Pure 100% black line art, zero gray lines, zero grayscale shading, zero gradients, zero solid black fill.

### BLOCK H — Negative Constraints
*Referência:* `art/prompts/NEGATIVE_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Proibições ativas:* No text, watermarks, signatures. No character design, cartoon, anime, manga, comic book style, superhero, mascot, cute/kawaii aesthetic. No photorealism, 3D render, medical diagrams. No rigid geometric mandalas or mechanical symmetry.
