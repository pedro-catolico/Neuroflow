## 3. PROMPT ARCHITECTURE COMPONENTS (Prompt Architecture v0.1)

### BLOCK A — Identity Constants
*Referência:* `art/prompts/MASTER_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Direção:* Contemplative Figurative identity. Crisp black line art on pure white paper.

### BLOCK B & C — Subject & Structural Setup
- **Subject Description:** Two humpback whales gliding serene and side-by-side through a calm ocean surface, where sea waves and air dissolve into fluid organic lines.
- **Framing & Camera Perspective:** Wide lateral shot capturing both majestic marine mammals gliding horizontally across the composition.
- **Pose / Attitude:** Peaceful, weightless gliding motion embodying fluid grace and deep calmness.

### BLOCK D — Neurographic Integration
- **Line Origin:** Organic lines wrapper around the whales' backs and emerge from the crests of ocean waves.
- **Spatial Connection:** Continuous neurographic contour lines seamlessly join the water currents and atmospheric air into a unified organic network.

### BLOCK E — Composition & Flow Variables
- **Compositional Type:** Fluxo lateral (horizontal lateral flow guiding eye movement across the page).
- **Dominant Flow:** Ondular (wave-like undulating rhythmic flow).
- **Negative Space Allocation:** Approximately 25-30% open white space, primarily in the sky/upper atmosphere zone.

### BLOCK F & G — Density, Complexity & Coloring Experience
- **Complexity Level:** Level 2 (Accessible adult complexity).
- **Line Density:** Medium line density.
- **Coloring Cells:** Primary bold black outlines defining the whales, paired with finer secondary lines in the wave patterns creating inviting, comfortable coloring cells.
- **Line Finish:** Pure 100% black line art, zero gray lines, zero grayscale shading, zero gradients, zero solid black fill.

### BLOCK H — Negative Constraints
*Referência:* `art/prompts/NEGATIVE_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Proibições ativas:* No text, watermarks, signatures. No character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, cute/kawaii aesthetic. No photorealism, 3D render, medical diagrams. No rigid geometric mandalas or mechanical symmetry.
