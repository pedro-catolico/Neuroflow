## 3. PROMPT ARCHITECTURE COMPONENTS (Prompt Architecture v0.1)

### BLOCK A — Identity Constants
*Referência:* `art/prompts/MASTER_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Direção:* Contemplative Figurative identity. Crisp black line art on pure white paper.

### BLOCK B & C — Subject & Structural Setup
- **Subject Description:** A serene adult female figure cupping her hands gently around a radiant botanical seed, from which tender sprout filaments and life-threads emerge.
- **Framing & Camera Perspective:** Close-medium shot centered on the figure's hands, serene lower face, and the glowing focal seed.
- **Pose / Attitude:** Tender, nurturing posture of peaceful reverence and mindful presence.

### BLOCK D — Neurographic Integration
- **Line Origin:** Delicate organic lines originate from the seed core and the gentle curves of her fingertips.
- **Spatial Connection:** Branching life-lines radiate outward through draped sleeve folds into atmospheric curves, connecting human touch to natural rebirth.

### BLOCK E — Composition & Flow Variables
- **Compositional Type:** Expansão central (central expansion with a bright, open focal core at the cupped hands).
- **Dominant Flow:** Centrífugo (centrifugal flow spreading outward from the central seed).
- **Negative Space Allocation:** Approximately 30-35% pure white negative space concentrated in the luminous center around the hands and upper margins.

### BLOCK F & G — Density, Complexity & Coloring Experience
- **Complexity Level:** Level 2 (Accessible adult complexity).
- **Line Density:** Baixa no centro (low line density centrally), aumentando suavemente para densidade média nas bordas.
- **Coloring Cells:** Large, clear, comfortable coloring areas in the hands and drapery, paired with elegant expanding sprout cells.
- **Line Finish:** Pure 100% black line art, zero gray lines, zero grayscale shading, zero gradients, zero solid black fill.

### BLOCK H — Negative Constraints
*Referência:* `art/prompts/NEGATIVE_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Proibições ativas:* No text, watermarks, signatures. No character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, cute/kawaii aesthetic. No glowing fairy magic, fantasy wands, sparkles, fantasy creature cliches. No photorealism, 3D render. No rigid geometric mandalas.

Contemplative Figurative adult coloring book page, Level 2 complexity, crisp black line art on pure white paper. A serene adult female figure gently cupping a botanical seed in her hands, close-medium shot. Central expansion composition with centrifugal flow, featuring a luminous core and 30-35% white negative space around the hands and upper perimeters.
