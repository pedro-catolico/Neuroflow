## 3. PROMPT ARCHITECTURE COMPONENTS (Prompt Architecture v0.1)

### BLOCK A — Identity Constants
*Referência:* `art/prompts/MASTER_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Direção:* Contemplative Figurative identity. Crisp black line art on pure white paper.

### BLOCK B & C — Subject & Structural Setup
- **Subject Description:** Two graceful adult human hands reaching toward each other from opposite sides, captured in the breathless moment just before fingertips touch.
- **Framing & Camera Perspective:** Dynamic diagonal close-up framing focused on the two approaching hands and the charged space between them.
- **Pose / Attitude:** Serene, poised gesture of reaching and profound spiritual connection, holding delicate anticipation without aggression.

### BLOCK D — Neurographic Integration
- **Line Origin:** Curving lines emerge from fingertips, palms, wrist contours, and flowing sleeves.
- **Spatial Connection:** Opposing energy streams radiate from each hand, intertwining in the central gap into an intricate web of harmonic resonance.

### BLOCK E — Composition & Flow Variables
- **Compositional Type:** Diagonal dinâmica (dynamic diagonal composition stretching across the page).
- **Dominant Flow:** Radial de dois focos opostos (dual opposing radial flows converging in the center).
- **Negative Space Allocation:** Approximately 25-30% pure white space preserved in open peripheral areas around the outer wrists.

### BLOCK F & G — Density, Complexity & Coloring Experience
- **Complexity Level:** Level 4 (Advanced adult complexity).
- **Line Density:** Alta no ponto de convergência central, diminuindo suavemente para densidade média nas bordas.
- **Coloring Cells:** Sharp, anatomical hand contours paired with rich interlocking wave cells in the convergence zone, designed for detailed coloring focus.
- **Line Finish:** Pure 100% black line art, zero gray lines, zero grayscale shading, zero gradients, zero solid black fill.

### BLOCK H — Negative Constraints
*Referência:* `art/prompts/NEGATIVE_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Proibições ativas:* No text, watermarks, signatures. No character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, cute/kawaii aesthetic. No glowing magical sparks, lightning bolts, sci-fi forcefields. No photorealism, 3D render. No rigid geometric mandalas.

Contemplative Figurative adult coloring book page, Level 4 advanced complexity, crisp black line art on pure white paper.
