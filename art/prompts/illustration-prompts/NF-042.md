## 3. PROMPT ARCHITECTURE COMPONENTS (Prompt Architecture v0.1)

### BLOCK A — Identity Constants
*Referência:* `art/prompts/MASTER_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Direção:* Contemplative Figurative identity. Crisp black line art on pure white paper.

### BLOCK B & C — Subject & Structural Setup
- **Subject Description:** A majestic universal World Tree whose expansive roots, mighty textured trunk, and swirling cosmic canopy embrace the full composition in an epic organic cycle.
- **Framing & Camera Perspective:** Panoramic full-page holistic perspective capturing the entire cosmic tree from deep subterranean roots to outer celestial branches.
- **Pose / Attitude:** Omnipresent, living, breathing botanical unity connecting earth, cosmos, and consciousness.

### BLOCK D — Neurographic Integration
- **Line Origin:** Curving lines emerge from subterranean taproots, twisting bark channels, and spiraling crown branches.
- **Spatial Connection:** Root tendrils and canopy boughs unfurl in a magnificent, continuous organic spiral that loops across the entire page, weaving subterranean soil, living timber, and atmospheric air into a unified cosmic tapestry.

### BLOCK E — Composition & Flow Variables
- **Compositional Type:** Espiral orgânica (universal holistic spiral composition embracing the entire canvas).
- **Dominant Flow:** Espiral (expansive cosmic spiral flow connecting roots, trunk, and sky).
- **Negative Space Allocation:** Approximately 20-25% pure white negative space in breathing intervals between spiral branch arms and root clusters.

### BLOCK F & G — Density, Complexity & Coloring Experience
- **Complexity Level:** Level 5 (Master adult complexity).
- **Line Density:** Alta (high line density with supreme linear clarity).
- **Coloring Cells:** Hundreds of distinct, printable coloring cells: strong structural trunk and root outlines supported by intricate foliage clusters and bark striations. Zero unprintable micro-clutter.
- **Line Finish:** Pure 100% black line art, zero gray lines, zero grayscale shading, zero gradients, zero solid black fill.

### BLOCK H — Negative Constraints
*Referência:* `art/prompts/NEGATIVE_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Proibições ativas:* No text, watermarks, signatures. No character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, cute/kawaii aesthetic. No esoteric religious symbols, tarot icons, Yggdrasil cartoon fantasy faces. No photorealism, 3D render. No rigid geometric mandalas or mechanical symmetry.
