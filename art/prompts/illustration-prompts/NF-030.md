## 3. PROMPT ARCHITECTURE COMPONENTS (Prompt Architecture v0.1)

### BLOCK A — Identity Constants
*Referência:* `art/prompts/MASTER_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Direção:* Contemplative Figurative identity. Crisp black line art on pure white paper.

### BLOCK B & C — Subject & Structural Setup
- **Subject Description:** An open book resting on a rustic wooden table beside a window with a gentle view of garden foliage.
- **Framing & Camera Perspective:** Eye-level intimate shot framing the open book in the foreground, wooden table surface, and window frame behind.
- **Pose / Attitude:** Still, quiet object presentation embodying solitude and a peaceful pause in daily life.

### BLOCK D — Neurographic Integration
- **Line Origin:** Delicate neurographic lines originate from the open book pages and the gentle light rays streaming through the window.
- **Spatial Connection:** The organic lines flow softly across the table surface and dissolve into defined visual contours towards the margins, seamlessly connecting object, room, and garden view.

### BLOCK E — Composition & Flow Variables
- **Compositional Type:** Active borders (framing line work near the outer margins, preserving a large, intentional central negative space).
- **Dominant Flow:** Centrípeto (drawing visual focus inward toward the resting book).
- **Negative Space Allocation:** 60-70% intentional negative space. This negative space has defined visual contours, acts as a primary active compositional element, and is fully intentional (not empty or unfinished).

### BLOCK F & G — Density, Complexity & Coloring Experience
- **Complexity Level:** Level 1 (Minimalist adult complexity).
- **Line Density:** Very low density, lightweight clean strokes.
- **Coloring Cells:** Clear, comfortable coloring regions (including at least three distinct coloring areas: the open book pages, the textured wooden table top, and the garden/window frame).
- **Line Finish:** Pure 100% black line art, zero gray lines, zero grayscale shading, zero gradients, zero solid black fill.

### BLOCK H — Negative Constraints
*Referência:* `art/prompts/NEGATIVE_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Proibições ativas:* No text, watermarks, signatures. No character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, cute/kawaii aesthetic. No photorealism, 3D render, medical diagrams. No rigid geometric mandalas or mechanical symmetry. No unfinished appearance, no blank forgotten spaces.
