## 3. PROMPT ARCHITECTURE COMPONENTS (Prompt Architecture v0.1)

### BLOCK A — Identity Constants
*Referência:* `art/prompts/MASTER_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Direção:* Contemplative Figurative identity. Crisp black line art on pure white paper.

### BLOCK B & C — Subject & Structural Setup
- **Subject Description:** An asymmetric drifting field of floating flower petals, fallen leaves, and swirling ripples scattered organically across tranquil water.
- **Framing & Camera Perspective:** Top-down overhead perspective capturing the floating botanical drift across the water surface without mechanical symmetry.
- **Pose / Attitude:** Drifting weightlessness, quiet surrender to slow water currents.

### BLOCK D — Neurographic Integration
- **Line Origin:** Curving contour lines emerge from petal edges, leaf ribs, and concentric water ripples.
- **Spatial Connection:** The contours of drifting petals dissolve into fluid eddy lines and currents, creating an interlocking tapestry of floral shapes and moving water.

### BLOCK E — Composition & Flow Variables
- **Compositional Type:** Campo denso (dense field composition covering the page in balanced, asymmetric organic rhythms).
- **Dominant Flow:** Ondular assimétrico (undulating wave-like current flow completely free of concentric circular symmetry).
- **Negative Space Allocation:** Approximately 20-25% pure white negative space preserved in open clearings of still water between drifting petal clusters.

### BLOCK F & G — Density, Complexity & Coloring Experience
- **Complexity Level:** Level 4 (Advanced adult complexity).
- **Line Density:** Alta (high line density composed of delicate, flowing contours).
- **Coloring Cells:** Rich network of individual closed petal, leaf, and ripple cells, comfortably dimensioned for meditative, multi-colored coloring.
- **Line Finish:** Pure 100% black line art, zero gray lines, zero grayscale shading, zero gradients, zero solid black fill.

### BLOCK H — Negative Constraints
*Referência:* `art/prompts/NEGATIVE_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Proibições ativas:* No text, watermarks, signatures. No character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, cute/kawaii aesthetic. No rigid geometric mandalas, mechanical kaleidoscope symmetry, concentric compass circles. No photorealism, 3D render. No solid black masses.

## 4. EXECUTABLE SPECIFIC PROMPT (Prompt Final em Inglês)

```text
Contemplative Figurative adult coloring book page, Level 4 advanced complexity, crisp black line art on pure white paper. An asymmetric drifting field of flower petals and leaves floating peacefully across tranquil water, top-down view. Fluid organic neurographic lines interweave petal contours, leaf veins, and water eddies into an unbroken living surface of deep meditation, completely free of concentric or mechanical symmetry. Dense field composition with undulating asymmetric flow, preserving 20-25% clean white negative space in quiet water clearings. Rich line work with clear hierarchy: well-defined botanical contours framing comfortable, delicate interior petal and ripple cells for immersive coloring. Clean 100% black lines, no gray, no shading, no gradients, no solid black fills. --no text, watermarks, signatures, character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, kawaii, rigid mandala, concentric circles, mechanical symmetry, photorealism, 3D.
```
