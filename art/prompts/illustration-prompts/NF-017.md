## 3. PROMPT ARCHITECTURE COMPONENTS (Prompt Architecture v0.1)

### BLOCK A — Identity Constants
*Referência:* `art/prompts/MASTER_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Direção:* Contemplative Figurative identity. Crisp black line art on pure white paper.

### BLOCK B & C — Subject & Structural Setup
- **Subject Description:** A sweeping mountain range transitioning seamlessly from smooth, barren rocky ridges on one side into lush, wooded valleys and foothills on the other.
- **Framing & Camera Perspective:** Wide panoramic landscape with a rising diagonal ridge line guiding the eye across the terrain transformation.
- **Pose / Attitude:** Majestic, enduring geological passage under morning illumination.

### BLOCK D — Neurographic Integration
- **Line Origin:** Curving lines originate from the sharp ridge peaks, stratified rock faces, and tree-covered slopes.
- **Spatial Connection:** Sparse, wide geological contours on the left progressively branch and weave into dense, organic forest canopies on the right in an unbroken metamorphic flow.

### BLOCK E — Composition & Flow Variables
- **Compositional Type:** Diagonal dinâmica (dynamic diagonal flow traversing from lower left to upper right).
- **Dominant Flow:** Lateral com transformação (progressive left-to-right evolution from simple to complex).
- **Negative Space Allocation:** Approximately 30% pure white space in the sky above the mountain crests.

### BLOCK F & G — Density, Complexity & Coloring Experience
- **Complexity Level:** Level 3 (Intermediate adult complexity).
- **Line Density:** Baixa à esquerda evoluindo para Média-Alta à direita.
- **Coloring Cells:** Wide, spacious cells on the rocky mountain slopes transitioning into medium, intricate foliage cells in the wooded valley.
- **Line Finish:** Pure 100% black line art, zero gray lines, zero grayscale shading, zero gradients, zero solid black fill.

### BLOCK H — Negative Constraints
*Referência:* `art/prompts/NEGATIVE_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Proibições ativas:* No text, watermarks, signatures. No character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, cute/kawaii aesthetic. No photorealism, 3D render. No rigid geometric mandalas or mechanical symmetry. No dramatic volcanic eruptions.

Contemplative Figurative adult coloring book page, Level 3 intermediate complexity, crisp black line art on pure white paper. A panoramic mountain range transitioning smoothly from sparse rocky peaks on the left into lush forested valleys on the right, dynamic diagonal framing.
