## 3. PROMPT ARCHITECTURE COMPONENTS (Prompt Architecture v0.1)

### BLOCK A — Identity Constants
*Referência:* `art/prompts/MASTER_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Direção:* Contemplative Figurative identity. Crisp black line art on pure white paper.

### BLOCK B & C — Subject & Structural Setup
- **Subject Description:** A cascading wilderness waterfall descending gracefully over tiered rock ledges into a pristine pool, framed by mossy boulders and lush river ferns.
- **Framing & Camera Perspective:** Vertical-diagonal landscape framing capturing the water descent from the top plateau down into the serene plunge pool below.
- **Pose / Attitude:** Continuous, graceful aqueous motion embodying natural purification and eternal renewal.

### BLOCK D — Neurographic Integration
- **Line Origin:** Curving lines originate from vertical falling water sheets, plunge pool eddies, layered rock shelves, and fern fronds.
- **Spatial Connection:** The vertical waterfall streams transition into undulating pool swirls and mossy rock contours, linking cascade, stone, and flora in an unbroken living loop.

### BLOCK E — Composition & Flow Variables
- **Compositional Type:** Diagonal dinâmica (dynamic diagonal cascade movement).
- **Dominant Flow:** Descendente + ondular (descending fall transitioning into undulating pool currents).
- **Negative Space Allocation:** Approximately 20-25% pure white negative space in foaming pool highlights and clear air above the fall.

### BLOCK F & G — Density, Complexity & Coloring Experience
- **Complexity Level:** Level 4 (Advanced adult complexity).
- **Line Density:** Média-Alta (medium-high line density with rich textural rhythm).
- **Coloring Cells:** Clear structural rock ledges paired with flowing vertical water ribbons and fern foliage, forming comfortable, distinct cells for pencils and markers.
- **Line Finish:** Pure 100% black line art, zero gray lines, zero grayscale shading, zero gradients, zero solid black fill.

### BLOCK H — Negative Constraints
*Referência:* `art/prompts/NEGATIVE_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Proibições ativas:* No text, watermarks, signatures. No character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, cute/kawaii aesthetic. No photorealism, 3D render. No dramatic storm floods, no rigid geometric mandalas or mechanical symmetry.

## 4. EXECUTABLE SPECIFIC PROMPT (Prompt Final em Inglês)

```text
Contemplative Figurative adult coloring book page, Level 4 advanced complexity, crisp black line art on pure white paper. A graceful waterfall cascading down tiered rock shelves into a tranquil mountain pool bordered by ferns, vertical diagonal framing. Fluid organic neurographic lines trace the falling water curtains and transition smoothly into undulating pool currents and mossy rock strata. Dynamic diagonal composition with descending and wave-like flow, preserving 20-25% clean white negative space in pool foam highlights and open air. Bold structural outlines on the rock ledges, enriched by comfortable vertical water and delicate fern cells for deep coloring engagement. Clean 100% black lines, no gray, no shading, no gradients, no solid black fills. --no text, watermarks, signatures, character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, kawaii, photorealism, 3D, mandalas, mechanical symmetry.
```
