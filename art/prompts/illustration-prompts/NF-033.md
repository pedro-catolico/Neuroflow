## 3. PROMPT ARCHITECTURE COMPONENTS (Prompt Architecture v0.1)

### BLOCK A — Identity Constants
*Referência:* `art/prompts/MASTER_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Direção:* Contemplative Figurative identity. Crisp black line art on pure white paper.

### BLOCK B & C — Subject & Structural Setup
- **Subject Description:** A graceful adult female figure seen from behind, standing atop a grassy hilltop and gently opening her arms to embrace the fresh mountain wind.
- **Framing & Camera Perspective:** Full-body back view centered on the hilltop, with open sky expanding all around.
- **Pose / Attitude:** Expansive, peaceful, liberating stance opening the chest and arms to the horizon.

### BLOCK D — Neurographic Integration
- **Line Origin:** Curving lines originate from her outstretched arms, windblown hair, and billowing dress hem.
- **Spatial Connection:** Broad sweeping wind currents radiate centrifugally from her fingertips and attire into the vast sky, connecting human posture with the atmosphere.

### BLOCK E — Composition & Flow Variables
- **Compositional Type:** Expansão central (central expansion radiating outward from the hilltop figure).
- **Dominant Flow:** Centrífugo (expansive centrifugal flow sweeping into open sky).
- **Negative Space Allocation:** Approximately 35% pure white negative space celebrating open air and breath.

### BLOCK F & G — Density, Complexity & Coloring Experience
- **Complexity Level:** Level 2 (Accessible adult complexity).
- **Line Density:** Baixa-Média (low-medium line density, light and airy).
- **Coloring Cells:** Clean, confident outlines defining the woman's silhouette, hillside grass, and broad flowing wind ribbons for effortless, joyful coloring.
- **Line Finish:** Pure 100% black line art, zero gray lines, zero grayscale shading, zero gradients, zero solid black fill.

### BLOCK H — Negative Constraints
*Referência:* `art/prompts/NEGATIVE_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Proibições ativas:* No text, watermarks, signatures. No character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, cute/kawaii aesthetic. No superhero poses, theatrical drama, fashion poses. No photorealism, 3D render. No rigid geometric mandalas.

Contemplative Figurative adult coloring book page, Level 2 complexity, crisp black line art on pure white paper. A serene adult female figure standing on a hilltop with arms gently outstretched to welcome the breeze, full-body back view. Central expansion composition with centrifugal flow, preserving 35% clean white negative space in the expansive mountain sky. Elegant black contours defining the woman and hilltop, accompanied by wide, comfortable wind cells for relaxing coloring. Clean 100% black lines, no gray, no shading, no gradients, no solid black fills. --no text, watermarks, signatures, character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, kawaii, superhero pose, fashion model, photorealism, 3D, mandalas, mechanical symmetry.
