## 3. PROMPT ARCHITECTURE COMPONENTS (Prompt Architecture v0.1)

### BLOCK A — Identity Constants
*Referência:* `art/prompts/MASTER_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Direção:* Contemplative Figurative identity. Crisp black line art on pure white paper.

### BLOCK B & C — Subject & Structural Setup
- **Subject Description:** A dignified adult male figure walking slowly in peaceful mindfulness down a secluded grove path, flanked by tall bamboo stalks that curve gracefully overhead.
- **Framing & Camera Perspective:** Frontal one-point perspective looking down the path toward the centered walking figure.
- **Pose / Attitude:** Quiet, unhurried, measured walking posture reflecting deep inner absorption and stillness.

### BLOCK D — Neurographic Integration
- **Line Origin:** Curving lines originate from vertical bamboo culms and overarching leafy canopies.
- **Spatial Connection:** The arched bamboo canopies spiral inward like a gentle vortex, drawing the viewer's gaze inexorably toward the calm walker in the quiet center.

### BLOCK E — Composition & Flow Variables
- **Compositional Type:** Espiral orgânica centrípeta (centripetal organic spiral drawing the eye toward the center).
- **Dominant Flow:** Centrípeto (inward-focusing flow framing the solitary walker).
- **Negative Space Allocation:** Approximately 25-30% pure white space focused in the clear central walking channel around the figure.

### BLOCK F & G — Density, Complexity & Coloring Experience
- **Complexity Level:** Level 3 (Intermediate adult complexity).
- **Line Density:** Baixa-Média no centro (spacious around figure), crescendo para alta densidade nas margens de bambu.
- **Coloring Cells:** Clean silhouette and attire lines for the walking man, framed by rhythmic slender bamboo cells for varied coloring.
- **Line Finish:** Pure 100% black line art, zero gray lines, zero grayscale shading, zero gradients, zero solid black fill.

### BLOCK H — Negative Constraints
*Referência:* `art/prompts/NEGATIVE_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Proibições ativas:* No text, watermarks, signatures. No character design, cartoon, anime, manga, martial arts comic, ninja, warrior, mascot, cute/kawaii aesthetic. No photorealism, 3D render. No rigid geometric mandalas or mechanical symmetry.

## 4. EXECUTABLE SPECIFIC PROMPT (Prompt Final em Inglês)

```text
Contemplative Figurative adult coloring book page, Level 3 intermediate complexity, crisp black line art on pure white paper. A serene adult male figure walking in mindful slowness down a path between tall curving bamboo groves that arch overhead, centered perspective. Fluid neurographic lines follow the bamboo stalks into an overarching gentle organic vortex that draws visual focus inward toward the walking figure. Centripetal spiral composition, with higher line density in the outer bamboo canopies and 25-30% open white negative space in the tranquil central clearing. Distinct black contours defining the walking man, complemented by elegant vertical reed cells for comfortable coloring. Clean 100% black lines, no gray, no shading, no gradients, no solid black fills. --no text, watermarks, signatures, character design, cartoon, anime, manga, warrior, ninja, martial arts, mascot, kawaii, photorealism, 3D, mandalas, mechanical symmetry.
```
