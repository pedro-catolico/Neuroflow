## 3. PROMPT ARCHITECTURE COMPONENTS (Prompt Architecture v0.1)

### BLOCK A — Identity Constants
*Referência:* `art/prompts/MASTER_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Direção:* Contemplative Figurative identity. Crisp black line art on pure white paper.

### BLOCK B & C — Subject & Structural Setup
- **Subject Description:** A majestic adult stag standing quietly in a forest clearing at dawn, its noble head turned attentively, framed by an expansive set of branching antlers.
- **Framing & Camera Perspective:** Centered medium shot of the solitary deer surrounded by atmospheric woodland mist and distant tree silhouettes.
- **Pose / Attitude:** Still, alert yet deeply calm posture capturing wild grace, noble presence, and pristine wilderness.

### BLOCK D — Neurographic Integration
- **Line Origin:** Curving lines originate from the branching antler tines, muscular shoulder contours, and textured meadow floor.
- **Spatial Connection:** The antler points organically branch outward, dissolving and dispersing into atmospheric mist lines and background tree canopies in irregular natural rhythms.

### BLOCK E — Composition & Flow Variables
- **Compositional Type:** Expansão central desestruturada (organic deconstructed central expansion).
- **Dominant Flow:** Centrífugo irregular (irregular centrifugal flow dispersing into morning fog).
- **Negative Space Allocation:** Approximately 30-35% pure white negative space in the misty perimeter margins.

### BLOCK F & G — Density, Complexity & Coloring Experience
- **Complexity Level:** Level 3 (Intermediate adult complexity).
- **Line Density:** Alta no centro focal do cervo, diminuindo para baixa densidade nas bordas nebulosas.
- **Coloring Cells:** Crisp, bold outlines for the stag's anatomy and main antlers, paired with comfortable, open coloring cells in the body, grass, and mist ribbons.
- **Line Finish:** Pure 100% black line art, zero gray lines, zero grayscale shading, zero gradients, zero solid black fill.

### BLOCK H — Negative Constraints
*Referência:* `art/prompts/NEGATIVE_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Proibições ativas:* No text, watermarks, signatures. No character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, cute/kawaii aesthetic. No Bambi-style cartooning, fantasy glowing antlers, hunting tropes. No photorealism, 3D render. No rigid geometric mandalas.

Contemplative Figurative adult coloring book page, Level 3 intermediate complexity, crisp black line art on pure white paper. A noble adult stag standing quietly in a misty forest clearing at dawn, centered medium framing. Deconstructed central expansion composition with irregular centrifugal flow, featuring high focal detail on the stag and 30-35% luminous white negative space in the misty periphery.
