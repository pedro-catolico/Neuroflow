## 3. PROMPT ARCHITECTURE COMPONENTS (Prompt Architecture v0.1)

### BLOCK A — Identity Constants
*Referência:* `art/prompts/MASTER_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Direção:* Contemplative Figurative identity. Crisp black line art on pure white paper.

### BLOCK B & C — Subject & Structural Setup
- **Subject Description:** Gentle ocean waves rolling over smooth coastal rocks in rhythmic layered tiers under clear sea air.
- **Framing & Camera Perspective:** Wide lateral coastal perspective capturing overlapping swells surging smoothly across rocky shoals.
- **Pose / Attitude:** Peaceful continuous coastal motion embodying oceanic breathing and timeless tranquility.

### BLOCK D — Neurographic Integration
- **Line Origin:** Curving lines originate from cresting surf ridges, foam lines, and the rounded contours of coastal boulders.
- **Spatial Connection:** Rhythmic wave contours sweep horizontally across the page, wrapping around rocks and extending into oceanic currents.

### BLOCK E — Composition & Flow Variables
- **Compositional Type:** Fluxo lateral (horizontal lateral flow traversing across the shore).
- **Dominant Flow:** Ondular (layered undulating rhythms echoing ocean swells).
- **Negative Space Allocation:** Approximately 30-35% pure white negative space in the calm open water intervals and open sea horizon.

### BLOCK F & G — Density, Complexity & Coloring Experience
- **Complexity Level:** Level 2 (Accessible adult complexity).
- **Line Density:** Baixa-Média (low-medium density).
- **Coloring Cells:** Broad, comfortable coloring cells formed by smooth wave tiers and rounded rocks, offering relaxing flowing strokes.
- **Line Finish:** Pure 100% black line art, zero gray lines, zero grayscale shading, zero gradients, zero solid black fill.

### BLOCK H — Negative Constraints
*Referência:* `art/prompts/NEGATIVE_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Proibições ativas:* No text, watermarks, signatures. No character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, cute/kawaii aesthetic. No violent crashing tsunamis, shipwrecks, stormy drama. No photorealism, 3D render. No rigid geometric mandalas.
