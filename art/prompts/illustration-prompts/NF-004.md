## 3. PROMPT ARCHITECTURE COMPONENTS (Prompt Architecture v0.1)

### BLOCK A — Identity Constants

*Direção:* Contemplative Figurative identity. Crisp black line art on pure white paper.

### BLOCK B & C — Subject & Structural Setup
- **Subject Description:** A poetic symbolic constellation of organic thought-lines and flowing interconnected bridges weaving across open space in continuous lyrical arcs, celebrating the beauty of consciousness without clinical or anatomical biology.
- **Framing & Camera Perspective:** Decentralized panoramic macro-field distributed evenly across the page, providing multiple points of contemplative visual discovery.
- **Pose / Attitude:** Harmonious, weightless suspension of symbolic visual nodes and curving filaments pulsing with serene poetic energy.

### BLOCK D — Neurographic Integration
- **Line Origin:** Curving contour lines emerge from luminous focal nodes and open elliptical junctions.
- **Spatial Connection:** Filamentous neurographic pathways weave smoothly across the entire composition, interconnecting secondary branching strands into an unbroken living tapestry of thought.

### BLOCK E — Composition & Flow Variables
- **Compositional Type:** Rede distribuída (decentralized distributed network across multiple visual nodes).
- **Dominant Flow:** Radial em múltiplos focos (multi-focal organic radial pathways guiding mindful exploration).
- **Negative Space Allocation:** Approximately 20-25% pure white negative space preserved in meandering open channels between active nodal clusters.

### BLOCK F & G — Density, Complexity & Coloring Experience
- **Complexity Level:** Level 5 (Master adult complexity).
- **Line Density:** Média-Alta a Alta with crystalline linear definition.
- **Coloring Cells:** Distinct line weight hierarchy: bold primary arcs anchoring major nodal conduits, supported by delicate interior secondary filaments, creating hundreds of comfortably enclosed, printable coloring cells without muddy clutter.
- **Line Finish:** Pure 100% black line art, zero gray lines, zero grayscale shading, zero gradients, zero solid black fill.
