## 3. PROMPT ARCHITECTURE COMPONENTS (Prompt Architecture v0.1)

### BLOCK A — Identity Constants
*Direção:* Contemplative Figurative identity. Crisp black line art on pure white paper.

### BLOCK B & C — Subject & Structural Setup
- **Subject Description:** A poetic symbolic constellation of organic thought-lines and flowing interconnected bridges weaving across open space in continuous lyrical arcs, celebrating the beauty of consciousness without clinical or anatomical biology. One large dominant organic junction anchors the composition, supported by 2–3 secondary branching bridge hubs at clearly different scales, plus many small open irregular junctions scattered as minor crossings — no two nodes alike, never five or more visually equivalent nodes.
- **Framing & Camera Perspective:** Decentralized panoramic macro-field distributed evenly across the page, providing multiple points of contemplative visual discovery. Long through-connections traverse the full composition, linking distant regions across quiet transition areas.
- **Pose / Attitude:** Harmonious, weightless suspension of varied organic knots and curving filaments pulsing with serene poetic energy. Nodes read as irregular thought knots and filament crossings — never as petals, blossoms, rosettes or flower heads.

### BLOCK D — Neurographic Integration
- **Line Origin:** Curving contour lines emerge from open irregular junctions and branching bridge hubs of varied organic shapes — asymmetric thought knots, filament crossings and overlapping arc clusters, never closed ovals, never ellipses, never petal-symmetric bursts.
- **Spatial Connection:** Filamentous neurographic pathways weave smoothly across the entire composition at several scales at once, interconnecting secondary branching strands and selective microfilament webs in chosen regions into an unbroken living tapestry of thought. Dense knotted areas alternate with breathing transition zones, so the viewer keeps discovering new connections while traveling across the page.

### BLOCK E — Composition & Flow Variables
- **Compositional Type:** Rede distribuída (decentralized distributed network across multiple visual nodes of deliberately varied scale: 1 large primary junction, 2–3 secondary hubs at different sizes, multiple small junctions).
- **Dominant Flow:** Radial em múltiplos focos (multi-focal organic pathways guiding mindful exploration through asymmetric convergence and divergence — filaments arrive and leave each junction at irregular angles and uneven spacing, never in even petal-like radial symmetry).
- **Negative Space Allocation:** Approximately 20-25% pure white negative space preserved in meandering open channels between active nodal clusters.

### BLOCK F & G — Density, Complexity & Coloring Experience
- **Complexity Level:** Level 5 (Master adult complexity) — built structurally through multiple scales, multiple connection levels, branching, overlap, microfilaments, secondary webs, varied cells, and alternating dense and breathing areas; never by filling the whole page uniformly with lines.
- **Line Density:** Média-Alta a Alta with crystalline linear definition.
- **Coloring Cells:** Explicit four-tier line weight hierarchy — PRIMARY: bold structural arcs and large bridges; SECONDARY: intermediate branches and mid-scale connections; TERTIARY: delicate interior filaments; MICRO: selective fine detail accents in chosen regions only — creating hundreds of varied, comfortably enclosed, printable coloring cells (large, medium and small) without muddy clutter and without uniform thin-line flatness.
- **Line Finish:** Pure 100% black line art, zero gray lines, zero grayscale shading, zero gradients, zero solid black fill.

### BLOCK H — Negative Constraints
*Proibições ativas:* No text, watermarks, signatures. No character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, cute/kawaii aesthetic. No scientific brain scans, medical histological slides, anatomical cross-sections, clinical biology diagrams. No rigid geometric mandalas or mechanical symmetry. No chaotic scribble, no muddy unprintable noise. No floral rosettes, no petal-like structures, no flower-shaped nodes, no botanical symmetry, no repeated blossom forms — abstract organic irregularity only.
