## 3. PROMPT ARCHITECTURE COMPONENTS (Prompt Architecture v0.1)

### BLOCK A — Identity Constants
*Referência:* `art/prompts/MASTER_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Direção:* Contemplative Figurative identity. Crisp black line art on pure white paper.

### BLOCK B & C — Subject & Structural Setup
- **Subject Description:** A serene adult human female seated quietly in peaceful meditation, embraced beneath a natural botanical arbor formed by living willow branches and interwoven climbing vines that gently open outward.
- **Framing & Camera Perspective:** Frontal eye-level medium shot framing the seated female figure within the protective botanical arch, with open luminous space beyond.
- **Pose / Attitude:** Still, grounded meditative sitting posture conveying deep serenity, safe refuge, and gentle protection without fantasy or fairy-tale tropes.

### BLOCK D — Neurographic Integration
- **Line Origin:** Organic lines originate from the cascading willow stems, hanging leafy vines, and the gentle folds of the seated figure's garments.
- **Spatial Connection:** The protective curving boughs encircle the figure and flow outward into open expansive arcs toward the outer atmosphere, joining human posture and natural sanctuary into an unbroken composition.

### BLOCK E — Composition & Flow Variables
- **Compositional Type:** Expansão central (central focal expansion with an organic protective dome).
- **Dominant Flow:** Centrífugo (centrifugal outward expansion radiating from the centered seated figure and opening boughs).
- **Negative Space Allocation:** Approximately 25-30% pure white negative space in the surrounding outer background, creating a bright, luminous atmosphere.

### BLOCK F & G — Density, Complexity & Coloring Experience
- **Complexity Level:** Level 3 (Intermediate adult complexity).
- **Line Density:** Média no centro (medium density forming the arbor and figure), transitioning to baixa e arejada nas bordas externas.
- **Coloring Cells:** Clearly delineated botanical leaves, cascading stems, and garment folds providing varied, comfortable closed coloring cells.
- **Line Finish:** Pure 100% black line art, zero gray lines, zero grayscale shading, zero gradients, zero solid black fill.

### BLOCK H — Negative Constraints
*Referência:* `art/prompts/NEGATIVE_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Proibições ativas:* No text, watermarks, signatures. No character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, cute/kawaii aesthetic. No fairy tale, magical fantasy cocoon, surreal creature, fairy wings. No photorealism, 3D render, medical diagrams. No rigid geometric mandalas or mechanical symmetry.

Contemplative Figurative adult coloring book page, Level 3 intermediate complexity, crisp black line art on pure white paper.
