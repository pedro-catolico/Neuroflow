## 3. PROMPT ARCHITECTURE COMPONENTS (Prompt Architecture v0.1)

### BLOCK A — Identity Constants
*Referência:* `art/prompts/MASTER_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Direção:* Contemplative Figurative identity. Crisp black line art on pure white paper.

### BLOCK B & C — Subject & Structural Setup
- **Subject Description:** An expansive meadow of tall flowering lavender spikes and slender ornamental grasses swaying in synchronized harmony beneath a gentle evening breeze.
- **Framing & Camera Perspective:** Low-angle close-medium landscape view immersing the viewer directly in the swaying field of stems and flower heads.
- **Pose / Attitude:** Graceful wind-driven sway capturing botanical rhythm and auditory silence (visual murmur).

### BLOCK D — Neurographic Integration
- **Line Origin:** Slender curving lines originate from the stems, floral bracts, and delicate grass tips.
- **Spatial Connection:** Parallel flowing lines mirror the invisible currents of the wind, weaving stems, blossoms, and air into an undulating field texture.

### BLOCK E — Composition & Flow Variables
- **Compositional Type:** Campo denso (dense field composition brimming with botanical texture).
- **Dominant Flow:** Ondular (wave-like undulating horizontal sway across the whole meadow).
- **Negative Space Allocation:** Approximately 20-25% intentional white negative space along the open upper horizon and between stem clusters.

### BLOCK F & G — Density, Complexity & Coloring Experience
- **Complexity Level:** Level 4 (Advanced adult complexity).
- **Line Density:** Média-Alta (medium-high line density composed of delicate, crisp lines).
- **Coloring Cells:** Rich array of slender floral spikes, grass ribbons, and open breeze channels, forming comfortable, highly rewarding cells for fine coloring.
- **Line Finish:** Pure 100% black line art, zero gray lines, zero grayscale shading, zero gradients, zero solid black fill.

### BLOCK H — Negative Constraints
*Referência:* `art/prompts/NEGATIVE_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Proibições ativas:* No text, watermarks, signatures. No character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, cute/kawaii aesthetic. No photorealism, 3D render. No rigid geometric mandalas or mechanical symmetry. No insects, no cartoon bees, no messy scrawl.

## 4. EXECUTABLE SPECIFIC PROMPT (Prompt Final em Inglês)

```text
Contemplative Figurative adult coloring book page, Level 4 advanced complexity, crisp black line art on pure white paper. A field of tall lavender stalks and wild grasses swaying together in the evening wind, low-angle immersive framing. Delicate organic neurographic lines follow the bending stems and floral spikes, capturing the undulating flow of the breeze across the meadow. Dense field composition with rhythmic wave-like flow, preserving 20-25% clean white negative space in the upper sky band. Exquisite line work with distinct hierarchy: firm contours defining major flower spikes paired with fine rhythmic grass ribbons forming comfortable coloring cells. Clean 100% black lines, no gray, no shading, no gradients, no solid black fills. --no text, watermarks, signatures, character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, kawaii, bees, insects, photorealism, 3D, mandalas, mechanical symmetry.
```
