## 3. PROMPT ARCHITECTURE COMPONENTS (Prompt Architecture v0.1)

### BLOCK A — Identity Constants
*Referência:* `art/prompts/MASTER_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Direção:* Contemplative Figurative identity. Crisp black line art on pure white paper.

### BLOCK B & C — Subject & Structural Setup
- **Subject Description:** A vast, immersive mountain forest of ancient tall pine trees with textured barks and needle canopies, through which a winding path weaves down the valley floor beneath a gentle veil of morning mist.
- **Framing & Camera Perspective:** Deep atmospheric multi-layered landscape perspective, with monumental foreground pine trunks framing middle-ground tree ridges and distant shrouded mountain slopes.
- **Pose / Attitude:** Still, silent, majestic forest sanctuary evoking deep introspective immersion and quiet mystery.

### BLOCK D — Neurographic Integration
- **Line Origin:** Organic lines originate from vertical tree bark textures, pine needle sprays, and the contour curves of the sinuous forest floor path.
- **Spatial Connection:** The vertical pine lines and undulating horizontal mist contours intertwine into a three-dimensional organic lattice, connecting earth, trees, and sky without turning into flat wallpaper clutter.

### BLOCK E — Composition & Flow Variables
- **Compositional Type:** Rede distribuída (distributed multi-layered environmental network composition).
- **Dominant Flow:** Vertical + ondulante (vertical tree trunk rhythm combined with undulating path and mist currents).
- **Negative Space Allocation:** Approximately 20-25% intentional pure white space preserved along the glowing misty forest path and atmospheric valley gaps between tree canopies.

### BLOCK F & G — Density, Complexity & Coloring Experience
- **Complexity Level:** Level 5 (Master adult complexity).
- **Line Density:** Alta (high line density executed with profound spatial layering).
- **Coloring Cells:** Hundreds of clearly delineated, printable coloring cells: strong vertical trunk silhouettes in the foreground establishing visual anchors, paired with delicate, comfortable needle clusters and misty path segments. Zero unprintable micro-scrawls or visual mud.
- **Line Finish:** Pure 100% black line art, zero gray lines, zero grayscale shading, zero gradients, zero solid black fill.

### BLOCK H — Negative Constraints
*Referência:* `art/prompts/NEGATIVE_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Proibições ativas:* No text, watermarks, signatures. No character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, cute/kawaii aesthetic. No photorealism, 3D render, photographic haze wash. No rigid geometric mandalas or mechanical symmetry. No flat chaotic tangles, no muddy indistinct lines.
