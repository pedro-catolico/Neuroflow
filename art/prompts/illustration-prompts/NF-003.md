## 3. PROMPT ARCHITECTURE COMPONENTS (Prompt Architecture v0.1)

### BLOCK A — Identity Constants
*Referência:* `art/prompts/MASTER_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Direção:* Contemplative Figurative identity. Crisp black line art on pure white paper.

### BLOCK B & C — Subject & Structural Setup
- **Subject Description:** A serene human female figure in a conscious breathing posture, with fluid lines of exhalation surrounding her.
- **Framing & Camera Perspective:** Center-focused medium shot emphasizing the torso, head, and surrounding exhalation flow.
- **Pose / Attitude:** Calm, mindful stance with a relaxed posture, embodying presence and stillness.

### BLOCK D — Neurographic Integration
- **Line Origin:** Organic breath-like arcs and irregular looping curves emerge from the chest area and radiate out into the negative space surrounding the figure.
- **Spatial Connection:** The breath lines weave outward into dense organic neurographic webs towards the page borders, linking the human figure seamlessly to the surrounding environment.

### BLOCK E — Composition & Flow Variables
- **Compositional Type:** Central expansion (inverted — dense line work at the outer borders, open and lightweight near the central chest area).
- **Dominant Flow:** Centrípeto (drawing visual focus inward toward the central breathing figure).
- **Negative Space Allocation:** Approximately 30-35% open white space concentrated around the central chest area.

### BLOCK F & G — Density, Complexity & Coloring Experience
- **Complexity Level:** Level 4
- **Line Density:** High line density at the outer margins, transitioning to clean, open space centrally.
- **Coloring Cells:** Varied cell sizes — larger open coloring spaces centrally, intricate cells towards the border patterns.
- **Line Finish:** Pure 100% black line art, zero gray lines, zero grayscale shading, zero gradients, zero solid black fill.

### BLOCK H — Negative Constraints
*Referência:* `art/prompts/NEGATIVE_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Proibições ativas:* No text, watermarks, signatures. No character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, cute/kawaii aesthetic. No photorealism, 3D render, medical diagrams. No rigid geometric mandalas or mechanical symmetry. No perfect circles, no concentric circular motifs.
