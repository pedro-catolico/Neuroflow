## 3. PROMPT ARCHITECTURE COMPONENTS (Prompt Architecture v0.1)

### BLOCK A — Identity Constants
*Referência:* `art/prompts/MASTER_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Direção:* Contemplative Figurative identity. Crisp black line art on pure white paper.

### BLOCK B & C — Subject & Structural Setup
- **Subject Description:** A lush botanical tapestry of overlapping tropical leaves, monstera fronds, and arching fern fronds displaying distinct organic ribbing and veins.
- **Framing & Camera Perspective:** Rich macro-layered frontal framing of overlapping foliage filling the visual plane with tactile elegance.
- **Pose / Attitude:** Still, vibrant botanical immersion celebrating the natural architecture of leaves.

### BLOCK D — Neurographic Integration
- **Line Origin:** Curving lines emerge from the central midribs and branching veins of monstera leaves and fern pinnae.
- **Spatial Connection:** The leaf veins naturally extend beyond leaf boundaries into continuous contour lines, connecting adjacent fronds into an unbroken living woven lattice.

### BLOCK E — Composition & Flow Variables
- **Compositional Type:** Campo denso (dense field composition rich in overlapping botanical layers).
- **Dominant Flow:** Ondular (undulating wave-like rhythms flowing through curved leaf blades).
- **Negative Space Allocation:** Approximately 20-25% pure white negative space in open interior leaf cutouts and serene background pauses.

### BLOCK F & G — Density, Complexity & Coloring Experience
- **Complexity Level:** Level 3 (Intermediate adult complexity).
- **Line Density:** Média (balanced medium line density).
- **Coloring Cells:** Generous, well-defined coloring cells formed by broad leaf surfaces and rhythmic vein divisions, ideal for smooth blending.
- **Line Finish:** Pure 100% black line art, zero gray lines, zero grayscale shading, zero gradients, zero solid black fill.

### BLOCK H — Negative Constraints
*Referência:* `art/prompts/NEGATIVE_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Proibições ativas:* No text, watermarks, signatures. No character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, cute/kawaii aesthetic. No photorealism, 3D render. No rigid geometric wallpaper, repetitive seamless tiles, mechanical mandalas.

## 4. EXECUTABLE SPECIFIC PROMPT (Prompt Final em Inglês)

```text
Contemplative Figurative adult coloring book page, Level 3 intermediate complexity, crisp black line art on pure white paper. A lush botanical composition of overlapping tropical monstera leaves and arching ferns, layered front-facing view. Fluid organic neurographic lines flow from central leaf veins to link adjacent fronds into an unbroken living vegetative tapestry. Dense field composition with undulating organic flow, preserving 20-25% clean white negative space in natural leaf fenestrations and background intervals. Bold black outlines for primary leaf shapes paired with clear, comfortable interior vein cells for relaxing tactile coloring. Clean 100% black lines, no gray, no shading, no gradients, no solid black fills. --no text, watermarks, signatures, character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, kawaii, wallpaper tiling, seamless pattern, photorealism, 3D, mandalas, mechanical symmetry.
```
