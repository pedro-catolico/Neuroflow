## 3. PROMPT ARCHITECTURE COMPONENTS (Prompt Architecture v0.1)

### BLOCK A — Identity Constants
*Referência:* `art/prompts/MASTER_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Direção:* Contemplative Figurative identity. Crisp black line art on pure white paper.

### BLOCK B & C — Subject & Structural Setup
- **Subject Description:** An ancient weathered stone arch bridge spanning a meandering woodland stream bordered by mossy banks and overhanging forest trees.
- **Framing & Camera Perspective:** Three-quarter perspective showing the sweeping curve of the bridge deck, the stone arch reflection in the water, and flanking woods.
- **Pose / Attitude:** Enduring architectural stability peacefully embracing flowing water and natural growth.

### BLOCK D — Neurographic Integration
- **Line Origin:** Curving lines originate from the masonry joints of the stone arch, water ripples beneath the span, and creeping shoreline roots.
- **Spatial Connection:** The semicircular stonework arch extends into undulating river currents and lateral tree roots, uniting built passage and wild forest into an unbroken composition.

### BLOCK E — Composition & Flow Variables
- **Compositional Type:** Campo denso (dense woodland landscape composition).
- **Dominant Flow:** Radial múltiplo (multiple focal flows centered on the bridge arch and stream curves).
- **Negative Space Allocation:** Approximately 25-30% pure white space maintained in the calm water reflections and open forest canopy gaps.

### BLOCK F & G — Density, Complexity & Coloring Experience
- **Complexity Level:** Level 3 (Intermediate adult complexity).
- **Line Density:** Média (balanced medium line density).
- **Coloring Cells:** Well-defined masonry cells in the stone bridge paired with flowing water ribbons and leaf clusters, providing diverse, comfortable coloring areas.
- **Line Finish:** Pure 100% black line art, zero gray lines, zero grayscale shading, zero gradients, zero solid black fill.

### BLOCK H — Negative Constraints
*Referência:* `art/prompts/NEGATIVE_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Proibições ativas:* No text, watermarks, signatures. No character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, cute/kawaii aesthetic. No medieval fantasy castles, trolls, fairy tale cottages. No photorealism, 3D render. No rigid geometric mandalas.

## 4. EXECUTABLE SPECIFIC PROMPT (Prompt Final em Inglês)

```text
Contemplative Figurative adult coloring book page, Level 3 intermediate complexity, crisp black line art on pure white paper. An ancient weathered stone arch bridge spanning a clear winding forest stream amidst woodland trees, three-quarter perspective. Organic neurographic lines flow from the stone masonry curves into meandering water currents and riverside tree roots, weaving bridge and forest into harmony. Dense field composition with multi-focal radial flow around the stone arch, preserving 25-30% clean white negative space in water reflections and canopy openings. Confident black contours for the bridge structure harmonized with comfortable enclosed water and foliage coloring cells. Clean 100% black lines, no gray, no shading, no gradients, no solid black fills. --no text, watermarks, signatures, character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, kawaii, fantasy castle, photorealism, 3D, mandalas, mechanical symmetry.
```
