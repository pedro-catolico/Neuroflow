## 3. PROMPT ARCHITECTURE COMPONENTS (Prompt Architecture v0.1)

### BLOCK A — Identity Constants
*Referência:* `art/prompts/MASTER_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Direção:* Contemplative Figurative identity. Crisp black line art on pure white paper.

### BLOCK B & C — Subject & Structural Setup
- **Subject Description:** An intricate symbolic marine ecosystem and microbiotic coral tapestry featuring branching coral fans, polyps, spiraling sea shells, and organic cellular clusters interwoven into a harmonious micro-universe.
- **Framing & Camera Perspective:** Macro wide-angle panorama distributed evenly across the page, providing equal optical clarity from edge to edge without a single overpowering central subject.
- **Pose / Attitude:** Still, organic, symphonic underwater growth showcasing the astonishing mathematical harmony of oceanic life.

### BLOCK D — Neurographic Integration
- **Line Origin:** Fluid organic contour lines originate from the ribbed ridges of coral branches, polyp clusters, and undulating marine filaments.
- **Spatial Connection:** Continuous fine neurographic bridges interconnect diverse coral clusters and marine nodes across the entire page, creating a unified living network rather than isolated floating fragments.

### BLOCK E — Composition & Flow Variables
- **Compositional Type:** Rede distribuída (distributed decentralized network composition across multiple visual nodes).
- **Dominant Flow:** Radial em múltiplos focos (multiple interconnected organic radial blooming centers guiding meditative eye wandering).
- **Negative Space Allocation:** Approximately 20-25% intentional open white space preserved within rhythmic breathing intervals and open internal fluid channels between dense coral clusters.

### BLOCK F & G — Density, Complexity & Coloring Experience
- **Complexity Level:** Level 5 (Master adult complexity).
- **Line Density:** Muito Alta (very high line density with crystalline linear precision).
- **Coloring Cells:** Hundreds of distinct, comfortably enclosed printable coloring cells with rigorous line hierarchy: medium primary contour lines anchoring major coral silhouettes and delicate, razor-sharp interior veins for deep focus. Zero unprintable micro-dots or muddy scrawls.
- **Line Finish:** Pure 100% black line art, zero gray lines, zero grayscale shading, zero gradients, zero solid black fill.

### BLOCK H — Negative Constraints
*Referência:* `art/prompts/NEGATIVE_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Proibições ativas:* No text, watermarks, signatures. No character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, cute/kawaii aesthetic. No photorealism, 3D render, clinical medical cross-sections. No rigid geometric mandalas or mechanical symmetry. No muddy lines, no chaotic unreadable scribble.
