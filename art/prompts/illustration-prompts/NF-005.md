## 3. PROMPT ARCHITECTURE COMPONENTS (Prompt Architecture v0.1)

### BLOCK A — Identity Constants
*Referência:* `art/prompts/MASTER_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Direção:* Contemplative Figurative identity. Crisp black line art on pure white paper.

### BLOCK B & C — Subject & Structural Setup
- **Subject Description:** A minimalist desert landscape of sweeping, gentle sand dunes undulating peacefully beneath an expansive, clear sky.
- **Framing & Camera Perspective:** Wide panoramic lateral landscape framing with low, elegant horizon line emphasizing vast open expanse.
- **Pose / Attitude:** Still, tranquil geological silence and breathing spaciousness.

### BLOCK D — Neurographic Integration
- **Line Origin:** Curving lines trace the rhythmic crests and gentle wind-swept slopes of the desert dunes.
- **Spatial Connection:** A few graceful, long-sweeping contour lines traverse horizontally, dissolving softly into the radiant clarity of the open atmosphere.

### BLOCK E — Composition & Flow Variables
- **Compositional Type:** Fluxo lateral (horizontal lateral flow guiding eye movement gently from left to right).
- **Dominant Flow:** Ondular (gentle, rhythmic wave-like undulation across the dune crests).
- **Negative Space Allocation:** Generous 50-60% pure white negative space dominating the vast upper sky and spacious open sand slopes.

### BLOCK F & G — Density, Complexity & Coloring Experience
- **Complexity Level:** Level 1 (Introductory adult complexity).
- **Line Density:** Baixa (very low density, unhurried linework).
- **Coloring Cells:** Broad, expansive, highly comfortable coloring cells with clean elegant contours, ideal for relaxing color gradients with pencils or pastel washes.
- **Line Finish:** Pure 100% black line art, zero gray lines, zero grayscale shading, zero gradients, zero solid black fill.

### BLOCK H — Negative Constraints
*Referência:* `art/prompts/NEGATIVE_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Proibições ativas:* No text, watermarks, signatures. No character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, cute/kawaii aesthetic. No photorealism, 3D render. No rigid geometric mandalas or mechanical symmetry. No cluttered rocks, no cacti clusters, no busy textures.

## 4. EXECUTABLE SPECIFIC PROMPT (Prompt Final em Inglês)

```text
Contemplative Figurative adult coloring book page, minimalist Level 1 complexity, crisp black line art on pure white paper. A serene desert landscape with gentle, sweeping sand dunes undulating beneath a vast open sky, wide panoramic lateral framing. Elegant, unhurried neurographic lines trace the dune ridges and dissolve smoothly into the clear atmosphere. Lateral composition with gentle ondular flow, featuring 50-60% intentional open white negative space in the sky and calm slopes. Clean, confident black outlines creating large, comfortable, welcoming coloring areas for an effortless meditative experience. Clean 100% black lines, no gray, no shading, no gradients, no solid black fills. --no text, watermarks, signatures, character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, kawaii, photorealism, 3D, mandalas, mechanical symmetry, clutter.
```
