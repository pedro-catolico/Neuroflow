## 3. PROMPT ARCHITECTURE COMPONENTS (Prompt Architecture v0.1)

### BLOCK A — Identity Constants
*Referência:* `art/prompts/MASTER_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Direção:* Contemplative Figurative identity. Crisp black line art on pure white paper.

### BLOCK B & C — Subject & Structural Setup
- **Subject Description:** A solitary ancestral oak tree standing with profound grounded authority, revealing both its deep subterranean root tapestry below the soil line and its high spreading crown branches above.
- **Framing & Camera Perspective:** Centered vertical full-tree view displaying the complete vertical anatomy of the tree: underground root network, massive weathered trunk, and leafy branch canopy.
- **Pose / Attitude:** Ancient, enduring, centered majesty embodying physical grounding and serene ecological stability.

### BLOCK D — Neurographic Integration
- **Line Origin:** Organic lines originate from the deep root tapestries, furrowed bark textures, and reaching branch tips.
- **Spatial Connection:** Root tendrils and branch contours flow continuously into the earth stratum and sky atmosphere, structuring the entire page into an unbroken living biological matrix.

### BLOCK E — Composition & Flow Variables
- **Compositional Type:** Expansão central (árvore invertida — weighted central expansion anchored at the subterranean base).
- **Dominant Flow:** Descendente (downward grounding gravitational flow anchoring the viewer's gaze into the root system).
- **Negative Space Allocation:** Approximately 25-30% open pure white space distributed around the upper branches and crown perimeters.

### BLOCK F & G — Density, Complexity & Coloring Experience
- **Complexity Level:** Level 3 (Intermediate adult complexity).
- **Line Density:** Alta na base terrosa, transicionando para densidade moderada e arejada no topo da copa.
- **Coloring Cells:** Distinct line weight hierarchy: bold defining lines for trunk and major roots, medium contours for branches, and delicate thin lines for foliage and subterranean striations, creating rewarding, comfortable coloring cells.
- **Line Finish:** Pure 100% black line art, zero gray lines, zero grayscale shading, zero gradients, zero solid black fill.

### BLOCK H — Negative Constraints
*Referência:* `art/prompts/NEGATIVE_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Proibições ativas:* No text, watermarks, signatures. No character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, cute/kawaii aesthetic. No photorealism, 3D render, cross-section medical diagrams. No rigid geometric mandalas or mechanical symmetry.

## 4. EXECUTABLE SPECIFIC PROMPT (Prompt Final em Inglês)

```text
Contemplative Figurative adult coloring book page, Level 3 intermediate complexity, crisp black line art on pure white paper. A majestic ancestral oak tree standing centered in timeless stability, revealing both its expansive subterranean root network and high spreading branch canopy, vertical full-tree framing. Continuous organic neurographic lines emerge from furrowed bark, root tendrils, and branch divisions, weaving earth and sky into a harmonious living structure. Central expansion composition with grounded descending flow, high line density in the root foundation transitioning to airy canopy branches, leaving 25-30% clean white negative space around the upper crown. Bold black trunk and root contours paired with fine foliage and soil striations forming comfortable coloring cells. Clean 100% black lines, no gray, no shading, no gradients, no solid black fills. --no text, watermarks, signatures, character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, kawaii, photorealism, 3D, mandalas, mechanical symmetry.
```
