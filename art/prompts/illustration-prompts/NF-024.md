## 3. PROMPT ARCHITECTURE COMPONENTS (Prompt Architecture v0.1)

### BLOCK A — Identity Constants
*Referência:* `art/prompts/MASTER_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Direção:* Contemplative Figurative identity. Crisp black line art on pure white paper.

### BLOCK B & C — Subject & Structural Setup
- **Subject Description:** An elegant minimalist Japanese Ikebana arrangement featuring a textured ceramic vase, an arching weathered branch, a single tender flower bud, and balanced botanical accents.
- **Framing & Camera Perspective:** Asymmetric close-up eye-level perspective placing the ceramic vase grounded in the lower quadrant, with the arching bough reaching dynamically into open space.
- **Pose / Attitude:** Still, poised zen simplicity celebrating mindful balance, intentional asymmetry, and quiet dignity.

### BLOCK D — Neurographic Integration
- **Line Origin:** Organic lines originate from the rounded contours of the ceramic vase, the weathered nodes of the curving branch, and the bud petals.
- **Spatial Connection:** Subtle fluid neurographic lines extend outward from the botanical tips into the surrounding atmosphere, establishing visual equilibrium between the solid object and empty space.

### BLOCK E — Composition & Flow Variables
- **Compositional Type:** Contraste de escala (scale contrast between the concentrated botanical centerpiece and vast surrounding space).
- **Dominant Flow:** Radial (organic radial reaching outward from the vase aperture into open space).
- **Negative Space Allocation:** Generous 35-40% intentional pure white space serving as an active zen element of stillness (*ma*).

### BLOCK F & G — Density, Complexity & Coloring Experience
- **Complexity Level:** Level 3 (Intermediate adult complexity).
- **Line Density:** Média (medium density concentrated within the vase textures, branch bark, and bud).
- **Coloring Cells:** Distinct line weight hierarchy: bold defining silhouettes for the ceramic vessel and main branch, medium lines for floral petals, and fine interior grain lines offering satisfying coloring cells.
- **Line Finish:** Pure 100% black line art, zero gray lines, zero grayscale shading, zero gradients, zero solid black fill.

### BLOCK H — Negative Constraints
*Referência:* `art/prompts/NEGATIVE_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Proibições ativas:* No text, watermarks, signatures. No character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, cute/kawaii aesthetic. No photorealism, 3D render, photographic gloss. No rigid geometric mandalas or mechanical symmetry. No cluttered tables, no unnecessary domestic clutter.

## 4. EXECUTABLE SPECIFIC PROMPT (Prompt Final em Inglês)

```text
Contemplative Figurative adult coloring book page, Level 3 intermediate complexity, crisp black line art on pure white paper. A poised Japanese Ikebana arrangement featuring an artisan ceramic vase, an arching weathered branch, and a single tender flower bud, asymmetric eye-level framing. Organic neurographic lines flow gracefully from the vase curves and branch nodes into open air currents, weaving physical object and surrounding atmosphere into harmonious equilibrium. Scale contrast composition with subtle radial flow, preserving 35-40% intentional serene white negative space (*ma*). Firm black contours defining the ceramic vase and botanical stems, complemented by fine organic grain and petal lines creating comfortable coloring cells. Clean 100% black lines, no gray, no shading, no gradients, no solid black fills. --no text, watermarks, signatures, character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, kawaii, photorealism, 3D, mandalas, mechanical symmetry.
```
