## 3. PROMPT ARCHITECTURE COMPONENTS (Prompt Architecture v0.1)

### BLOCK A — Identity Constants
*Referência:* `art/prompts/MASTER_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Direção:* Contemplative Figurative identity. Crisp black line art on pure white paper.

### BLOCK B & C — Subject & Structural Setup
- **Subject Description:** A graceful adult female figure gently unloosening an intricate silk ribbon with her hands, freeing the knot in a peaceful gesture of liberation.
- **Framing & Camera Perspective:** Centered medium shot focused on her hands, serene posture, and the expanding loops of the untied ribbon.
- **Pose / Attitude:** Mindful, tender gesture of untying and letting go, embodying relief, release, and freedom.

### BLOCK D — Neurographic Integration
- **Line Origin:** Curving lines originate from the unloosening ribbon loops, fingertips, and garment sleeves.
- **Spatial Connection:** The loosened ribbon unravels outward from the central hands into expansive, sweeping neurographic ribbons that loop gracefully toward the outer page edges.

### BLOCK E — Composition & Flow Variables
- **Compositional Type:** Expansão central (central expansion with a spacious, liberated focal clearing at the center).
- **Dominant Flow:** Centrífugo (centrifugal flow uncurling outward from the freed knot).
- **Negative Space Allocation:** Approximately 25-30% pure white negative space opening up around the center hands and ribbon coils.

### BLOCK F & G — Density, Complexity & Coloring Experience
- **Complexity Level:** Level 4 (Advanced adult complexity).
- **Line Density:** Baixa no centro (liberated center), crescendo para média-alta densidade nas faixas fluidas periféricas.
- **Coloring Cells:** Distinct line weight hierarchy: bold outlines for hands and primary ribbon edges, paired with fine flowing interior lines creating rewarding coloring cells.
- **Line Finish:** Pure 100% black line art, zero gray lines, zero grayscale shading, zero gradients, zero solid black fill.

### BLOCK H — Negative Constraints
*Referência:* `art/prompts/NEGATIVE_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Proibições ativas:* No text, watermarks, signatures. No character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, cute/kawaii aesthetic. No theatrical fantasy magic, glowing ropes, fairy tale tropes. No photorealism, 3D render. No rigid geometric mandalas.

Contemplative Figurative adult coloring book page, Level 4 advanced complexity, crisp black line art on pure white paper. A serene adult female figure mindfully untying an intricate flowing ribbon in her hands, centered medium shot. Central expansion composition with centrifugal flow, creating a spacious liberated core with 25-30% clean white negative space. Defined black contours on hands and ribbon bands, harmonized with comfortable interior linear cells for immersive coloring.
