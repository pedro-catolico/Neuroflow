## 3. PROMPT ARCHITECTURE COMPONENTS (Prompt Architecture v0.1)

### BLOCK A — Identity Constants
*Referência:* `art/prompts/MASTER_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Direção:* Contemplative Figurative identity. Crisp black line art on pure white paper.

### BLOCK B & C — Subject & Structural Setup
- **Subject Description:** A welcoming rural rustic veranda with a wooden rocking chair, climbing blooming honeysuckle vines on the porch pillar, overlooking rolling hills at sunset.
- **Framing & Camera Perspective:** Eye-level cozy architectural perspective from the porch looking out toward the evening horizon.
- **Pose / Attitude:** Quiet, welcoming stillness, peaceful homecoming, and harmonious closure of the coloring journey.

### BLOCK D — Neurographic Integration
- **Line Origin:** Curving lines originate from the curved rockers and slats of the chair, wooden floorboards, porch pillar, and twisting flowering vines.
- **Spatial Connection:** The architectural lines of the porch deck flow smoothly into the rolling hill contours and evening breeze in the distance, uniting shelter and nature.

### BLOCK E — Composition & Flow Variables
- **Compositional Type:** Fluxo lateral (horizontal lateral flow guiding the gaze comfortably across the porch to the sunset).
- **Dominant Flow:** Ondular (gentle undulating wave flow through hills and vines).
- **Negative Space Allocation:** Approximately 25-30% clean white space in the luminous sunset sky and calm porch spaces.

### BLOCK F & G — Density, Complexity & Coloring Experience
- **Complexity Level:** Level 3 (Intermediate adult complexity).
- **Line Density:** Média (balanced medium line density).
- **Coloring Cells:** Distinct wooden chair and porch outlines paired with comfortable leaf, flower, and rolling hill cells, designed for warm, peaceful coloring.
- **Line Finish:** Pure 100% black line art, zero gray lines, zero grayscale shading, zero gradients, zero solid black fill.

### BLOCK H — Negative Constraints
*Referência:* `art/prompts/NEGATIVE_PROMPT.md` v0.2.1  
*Proibições ativas:* No text, watermarks, signatures. No character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, cute/kawaii aesthetic. No photorealism, 3D render. No cluttered domestic debris, no rigid geometric mandalas or mechanical symmetry.

## 4. EXECUTABLE SPECIFIC PROMPT (Prompt Final em Inglês)

```text
Contemplative Figurative adult coloring book page, Level 3 intermediate complexity, crisp black line art on pure white paper. A peaceful country veranda with a classic wooden rocking chair and climbing flowering vines around a porch post, overlooking rolling hills at sunset, eye-level cozy framing. Fluid organic neurographic lines connect the chair rockers and wooden deck to the undulating hillside horizons and gentle evening breeze. Lateral composition with peaceful undulating flow, preserving 25-30% clean white negative space in the glowing sky. Confident black structural outlines for the rocking chair and porch, complemented by comfortable, closed botanical and landscape coloring cells. Clean 100% black lines, no gray, no shading, no gradients, no solid black fills. --no text, watermarks, signatures, character design, cartoon, anime, manga, mascot, kawaii, photorealism, 3D, mandalas, mechanical symmetry.
```
